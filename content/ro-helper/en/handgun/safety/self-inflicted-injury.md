## What this is

Bodily injury from careless gun handling, including from uncontrolled **recoil reaction** or a violation of **muzzle** direction — **DQ** under **10.3.3** within **10.3**.

## RO algorithm

1. **Observation:** assess the danger on the scene; call match medical staff if needed.
2. **Warning/Stop:** "Stop"; first aid within your competence; gun to a safe state under **RO** / **RM** control.
3. **RM call:** notify the **RM** and **MD**; the **RM** issues a **DQ** under **10.3.3** if the grounds match the primary rules.
4. **Document:** incident report; **DQ** record; preserve the data for investigation and the **arbitration fee** per the regulations.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* Even a minor wound from a discharge or unsafe manipulation usually rules out continuation for safety reasons — the **RM** decides under **10.3**.
* Do not mix medical aid with a rules debate — person first, **10.3.3** second.

## Local (FPSU)

Chapter XI. Federation safety service reports for every injury incident at the match.
