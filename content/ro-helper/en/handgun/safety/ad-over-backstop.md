## What this is

A shot whose round crosses the **backstop / berm** line upward or beyond the berm — an **accidental discharge** under **10.4.1**. 2026 logic: **DQ regardless of distance** to the shooter once the event qualifies under **10.4.1**.

## RO algorithm

1. **Stop:** **"STOP!"** when you hear a discharge with a high **muzzle** angle, or when witnesses report a round leaving the bay over the **berm**.
2. **Safety:** control the competitor and the **muzzle**; halt the COF on the bay.
3. **Inspect:** check the berm and surroundings for impact marks; interview witnesses; unload under supervision; the **RM** confirms the trajectory if needed.
4. **Document:** **DQ** per **10.4.1**; record for MD / range.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Criterion:** an impact on an object behind the **berm** (tree, structure) when the round leaves the safe sector is typically **10.4.1**.
* **Intent:** "I didn't mean to" does not reverse the **DQ** if the event meets **10.4.1**.

## Common RO mistakes

* Dismissing a "shot in the air" without checking the berm and witnesses.
