## What this is

Pointing the **muzzle** during the draw or holstering so that the **muzzle** ends up more than **1 m** from the shooter on the horizontal plane (the conceptual circle around the feet) — **DQ** under **10.5.2**.

## RO algorithm

1. **Stop:** **"STOP!"** at the moment the unsafe angle / distance is observed.
2. **Safety:** lock the shooter visually and verbally; do not allow them to reach the **trigger**; **muzzle** to a safe direction.
3. **Unload:** order the shooter to unload under RO control, **chamber check** after the empty declaration.
4. **Document:** **RM** — register **DQ** per **10.5.2**.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Angle:** a **muzzle** parallel to the ground during draw / holstering is normally a **10.5.2** violation, even if the distance is hard to estimate by eye.
* **COF context:** outside an officially supervised stage these manipulations cannot be excused as "convenience".

## Common RO mistakes

* Hesitation without recording the fact; if in doubt, escalate to the **RM** immediately after **STOP**.
