## What this is

**Active ear protection** or plugs fall off or shift so that the ears are effectively unprotected during the **engagement** — an event under **5.4.4**. 2026 logic: **stop** and **reshoot** when effective hearing protection is lost during the COF (analogous to eye protection).

## RO algorithm

1. **Assess:** spot the loss or improper seating of the **active ear protection** / plugs before the next shot.
2. **Action:** **"STOP!"**; **muzzle control**; do not allow a shot before protection is restored.
3. **Communicate:** explain the stop; after a safe unload, allow the shooter to refit the protection; remind them of the acoustic-injury risk to themselves and the staff.
4. **Document:** issue a **reshoot** by **RM** decision / match procedure; do not apply a procedural penalty instead of a **reshoot** in this scenario.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* If **active ear protection** has only switched off (battery), but mechanically still covers the ears, a stop is not mandatory; the shooter can continue at their own risk if match policy and the primary rules allow.
* An ear cup falling to the ground during movement is the textbook trigger for **STOP**.

## Common RO mistakes

* Charging a penalty instead of a **reshoot** after a confirmed loss of hearing protection during a string.
