## What this is

2026 logic: **any drop of a loaded gun within a COF — DQ** under **10.5.3**; an unloaded drop within a COF is **also a DQ**, except for the narrow cases described explicitly in the Jan 2026 primary rules (interpreted by the **RM**). Outside a COF — **10.5.14**. **The shooter never** picks up the **dropped gun** themselves under this protocol.

## RO algorithm

1. **Stop:** **"STOP!"** every time the gun is dropped — first secure the surroundings.
2. **Isolate:** the shooter has no contact with the gun; spectators are moved away.
3. **Safe pickup:** the **RO** performs the lift and **unload**; **chamber check**: a magazine in the well means treat the system as loaded until verified.
4. **Document:** if the event is inside a COF, default to **DQ** under **10.5.3**; if the primary rules acknowledge an exception (rare and explicit), it stands only on a written **RM** decision. After "Range is Clear" outside an **SA** — reference **10.5.14**. A drop inside a closed transport case without opening the gun is normally not a "dropped gun" in the COF sense.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* "Empty magazine, but inserted" ≠ unloaded gun for safety purposes.
* A warning instead of a **DQ** is only valid if the primary rules and the **RM** explicitly cover the specific scenario.

## Common RO mistakes

* Confusing an unloaded gun with a "magazine in, chamber unverified" configuration.
