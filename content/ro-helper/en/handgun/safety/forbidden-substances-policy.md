## What this is

A competitor at the match under the influence of alcohol or substances that reduce **engagement** safety — under **10.6.3**; consequence — **disqualification (DQ)** and removal from the venue under **10.3** / **10.6**.

## RO algorithm

1. **Observation:** record objective signs (smell, speech, coordination); do not make medical conclusions — only flag the risk.
2. **Warning/Stop:** 2026 logic: **immediate removal** from the line, no contact with firearms or ammunition until the **RM** / **MD** arrives.
3. **RM call:** **RM** and **MD** conduct the conversation / verification policy per the primary rules; the **DQ** is announced by the **RM** under **10.6.3**.
4. **Document:** official **DQ** record, time, the person, who was informed; removal from the range area is decided by the **MD**.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* "Zero tolerance" alcohol policy at the venue follows the **MD** briefing.
* Medication that affects reaction time must be declared to the **RM** by the shooter before the **engagement**; the **RM** may bar participation on safety grounds without invoking other sections in this card.

## Common RO mistakes

* "Negotiating" to skip stages instead of issuing the formal **DQ** under **10.6.3**.
