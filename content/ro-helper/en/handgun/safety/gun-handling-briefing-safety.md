## What this is

Touching a firearm during the WSB briefing / walkthrough outside a permitted area or without the **RO**'s command creates a safety incident. The **RO** applies a **legal protocol**: documentation, evidence, **RM**, and if needed an **arbitration** / decision under **11.1**; the actual sanction is set by the match's primary rules.

## RO algorithm (legal protocol)

1. **Identify:** stop incorrect manipulations immediately (drawing from the holster, aiming, dry-fire outside an **SA**); secure **muzzle control** and distance.
2. **Document:** record witnesses, time, briefing stage; if needed, note the official video for later review.
3. **Escalate:** call the **RM** for the disqualification decision or another sanction per the primary rules; if the shooter contests the **RO**'s actions — open the **arbitration** procedure under **11.1**.
4. **Record decision:** enter the event in the match log, update the **official scoreboard** as the **RM** instructs; explain the outcome to the shooter in writing.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* During the briefing, minimise any movement of hands toward the firearm; "adjusting the holster" without drawing is acceptable only if it does not distract from squad safety.
* **Recoil reaction** drills and demonstration shots are allowed only inside an **SA** or on a clear command from the responsible match officer.

## Local (FPSU)

Chapter XI §5. Imitating an **engagement** during a briefing without authorisation is a textbook scenario for immediate escalation to the **RM**.
