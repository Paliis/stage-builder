## What this is

**Holstering** while **moving** between positions. 2026 logic: **unconditional DQ** under **10.5.11** (even if the pistol is empty — the primary rules and the match procedure still require a stop before holstering).

## RO algorithm

1. **Stop:** **"STOP!"** when the **muzzle** approaches the holster during a step / slide / position change.
2. **Safety:** the shooter completes the **movement** and freezes; **muzzle** at the targets; hands visible.
3. **Unload:** after **STOP** — controlled unload; **holster** only when the gun is safe and the shooter is static.
4. **Document:** **RM** — **DQ** per **10.5.11**.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* Holstering is allowed only when the shooter is **static** after finishing the engagement, if the WSB authorises it.
* Combining motion and a holster sharply increases the risk of a **recoil reaction** in an unsafe direction and self-injury.

## Local (FPSU)

Chapter XI §5. Under FPSU rules this is a no-exception **DQ** at FPSU-sanctioned matches.
