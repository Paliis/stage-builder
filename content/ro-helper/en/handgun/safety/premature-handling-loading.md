## What this is

Unauthorised manipulation of the firearm or ammunition before the **"Load and Make Ready"** command (chambering, magazine seating, slide work outside the permitted procedure). 2026 logic: if the **muzzle** / pistol **leaves the holster** or the gun leaves the **case** without a command — **unconditional DQ** under **10.5.1**.

## RO algorithm

1. **Stop:** **"STOP!"** at any forbidden manipulation or removal of the gun.
2. **Safety:** **muzzle control**; no further loading; finger off the trigger.
3. **Unload:** unload under supervision; show clear chamber.
4. **Document:** **RM** — **DQ** per **10.5.1**; record whether the gun left the holster / **case** and whether ammunition was touched.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* An attempt to seat a magazine in the well without locking it can already count as starting the load before the command.
* After **STOP**, do not relax **muzzle control** until unloading is complete.

## Local (FPSU)

Chapter XI §5. Touching ammunition on the firing line before the **RO** command is normally treated as a gross safety violation under match policy.
