## What this is

A "facing uprange" start: **drawing** the pistol or contacting the gun / holster before the **muzzle** has crossed the **90-degree line** relative to the target engagement line (i.e. before the body has safely rotated). Sanction — **DQ** under **10.5.2** (unsafe **muzzle angles**).

## RO algorithm

1. **Stop:** **"STOP!"** at any premature **draw** or grip of the pistol while still facing uprange.
2. **Safety:** the shooter completes the rotation under command; **muzzle** stays holstered until the safe angle is reached; no shots.
3. **Unload:** after the COF stop — unload, show clear chamber; **holster** per the procedure.
4. **Document:** **RM** — **DQ** per **10.5.2**; record whether the **muzzle** had cleared the holster before crossing the **90-degree line**.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Sequence:** rotate the body first so the **muzzle** clears the holster pointed downrange in a permitted sector — never to the rear.
* **Threshold:** even a partial lift of the pistol from the holster to the rear before crossing the **90-degree line** is a **muzzle-angle** violation.

## Common RO mistakes

* Allowing the shooter to "grip the pistol" before the feet and torso have rotated.
