## What this is

A **dropped gun** during COF preparation ("Load and Make Ready", etc.): loss of control, contact with a surface. 2026 logic: **any drop of a loaded gun within a COF — DQ** under **10.5.3**; an unloaded drop within a COF is normally **also a DQ**, except for the narrow exceptions in the primary rules.

## RO algorithm

1. **Stop:** immediate **"STOP!"** the moment the pistol touches the ground / table or leaves the shooter's hand.
2. **Isolate:** strictly forbid the shooter from picking up the **dropped gun**; have them step back, hands visible; bystanders out of the muzzle line.
3. **Safe pickup:** **RO** picks up the pistol (frame / slide, no trigger contact), **muzzle** to the berm; **unload** under control; **chamber check** plus magazine well check.
4. **Document:** **RM** issues **DQ** per **10.5.3**; record time, witnesses, and whether a magazine / a chambered round was present.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* The rule does not depend on whether the magazine seating was finished.
* Even a slide of the pistol off the table without a "full" fall counts as loss of control → **10.5.3**.

## Common RO mistakes

* Letting the shooter "quickly grab" the pistol — never allowed; pickup risks a discharge.
