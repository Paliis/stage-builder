## What this is

A **dropped gun** while moving between positions, or any time the gun loses contact with the shooter and touches a surface during a COF — **DQ** under **10.5.3**.

## RO algorithm

1. **Stop:** instant **"STOP!"** when the pistol falls or separates from the body and contacts the floor / a prop.
2. **Isolate:** the shooter freezes; no touching the **dropped gun**; the **RO** positions themselves between the shooter and the firearm — the shooter must not pick it up.
3. **Safe pickup:** **RO** lifts the pistol, **muzzle** in a safe sector; **unload**; **chamber check**; avoid pointing the **muzzle** at yourself or the shooter during the lift.
4. **Document:** **RM** — **DQ** per **10.5.3**; if the **STOP** was caused only by the shooter falling without losing the gun, the **RM** decides on a **reshoot** without invoking other rules from this card.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* If the shooter falls but the pistol stays in their hand pointed in a safe direction, this is *not* a **dropped gun** scenario under **10.5.3**.
* Any contact between the gun and the ground after a loss of grip falls under **10.5.3**.

## Local (FPSU)

Chapter XI §5. A drop during movement is treated as unreliable retention; the **RO** uses the standard pickup protocol.
