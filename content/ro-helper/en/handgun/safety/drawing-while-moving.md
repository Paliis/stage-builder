## What this is

**Drawing** the pistol from the holster while **moving** (any step, slide, or change of shooting position without a full stop). 2026 logic: **unconditional DQ** under **10.5.11**.

## RO algorithm

1. **Stop:** **"STOP!"** the moment you see a **draw** while **moving**.
2. **Safety:** the shooter stops; **muzzle** to a safe sector only; no magazine grab and no further engagement.
3. **Unload:** standard unload and show clear; **holster** after safety is confirmed.
4. **Document:** **DQ** per **10.5.11**; **RM** records the incident.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Movement:** even a single step or a level change (standing up / kneeling) while working with the holster is **moving** for this rule.
* **Start signals:** do not give starts that effectively force a step at the same time as the **draw**.

## Common RO mistakes

* Allowing "move and **draw**" on the way out of a position without a clear stop before holster work.
