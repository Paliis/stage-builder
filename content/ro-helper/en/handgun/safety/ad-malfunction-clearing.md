## What this is

An **accidental discharge** while clearing a malfunction (stripping a magazine, cycling the slide, etc.) — **DQ** under **10.4.5** (2026 logic: a shot fired while clearing a malfunction is a **DQ**).

## RO algorithm

1. **Stop:** **"STOP!"** the moment the gun fires during malfunction-clearing manipulation.
2. **Safety:** prohibit further "yanking" of the slide; control the **muzzle** direction; finger off the trigger.
3. **Inspect:** safe unload; recover the round(s) that caused the malfunction; check the impact area. Even if the competitor claims the intent was to engage a target, a shot while wrestling with a stoppage is qualified under **10.4.5**.
4. **Document:** **RM** issues **DQ** per **10.4.5**.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Engagement claim:** a shot pointed at a target while the slide was being racked is still **10.4.5**, not a normal scoring shot.
* **Muzzle control:** do not change grip "for the shooter's comfort" before the inspection is complete.

## Common RO mistakes

* Letting it slide because "the front sight was on the target".
