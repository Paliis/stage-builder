## What this is

A shot fired outside a course of fire, when the gun should not be in a firing condition (waiting area, parking lot, range bag, etc.) — an **accidental discharge** under **10.4.7**, **DQ**.

## RO algorithm

1. **Stop:** **"STOP!"**; isolate the competitor from the crowd.
2. **Safety:** **muzzle** to a safe sector / berm; no further manipulation.
3. **Inspect:** unload; establish where exactly the discharge happened (which area of the venue); inspect the bag / case if the shot occurred there; document for the **RM**.
4. **Document:** **DQ** per **10.4.7**; no "warnings" instead of disqualification.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Match venue:** the rule applies across the entire competition area, including zones where engagement is not allowed.
* **Severity:** a quiet discharge or no injuries does not reduce the **DQ** under **10.4.7**.

## Common RO mistakes

* Issuing a warning instead of calling the **RM** for a **DQ**.
