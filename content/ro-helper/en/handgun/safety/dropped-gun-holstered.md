## What this is

The pistol falls out of the holster together with its mount, or because of a hardware failure, during a COF — a **dropped gun**, **DQ** under **10.5.3** (2026 logic includes loaded and typical match configurations within a COF).

## RO algorithm

1. **Stop:** **"STOP!"** immediately.
2. **Isolate:** the shooter does not touch the holster / pistol on the ground; treat the configuration as potentially loaded until a **chamber check**.
3. **Safe pickup:** **RO** lifts the holster with the pistol, or them separately, using a safe procedure; **muzzle** to the berm; **unload**; show an empty chamber.
4. **Document:** **RM** — **DQ** per **10.5.3**; record the state of the mount for the **MD** report.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* A broken holster shell does not waive the **DQ** in a COF — equipment is the shooter's responsibility.
* A holster ejection inside a COF is **always** processed under **10.5.3**, no "hardware excuse".

## Common RO mistakes

* Letting the shooter adjust the belt or holster on the ground before the **RO** arrives.
