## What this is

**Eye protection** shifts or falls off during the stage — a violation of the eye-protection requirements under **5.4.4**. 2026 logic: **mandatory stop** and **reshoot** for any accidental loss during a COF.

## RO algorithm

1. **Assess:** instantly recognise that the eyes are not protected per the rules (glasses removed / fallen off / held by hand without proper seating).
2. **Action:** **"STOP!"** command; **muzzle control**; the shooter does not engage and does not adjust glasses "on the move" mid-string.
3. **Communicate:** explain the cause of the stop; after a safe unload, allow the shooter to refit **eye protection**; check for injuries.
4. **Document:** issue a **reshoot** per the match procedure and **RM** decision; record the moment of the event. If there are signs of intentional removal, hand it to the **RM** for a primary-rules decision (no other rule references in this card).

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* Continuing the stage while holding glasses with one hand is not allowed.
* **Recoil reaction** and abrupt motion are common dislodgement causes — check frame fit during the briefing.

## Local (FPSU)

Chapter IV §4. Eye protection is a priority; ignoring a loss of glasses on the line is a serious **RO** dereliction of duty.
