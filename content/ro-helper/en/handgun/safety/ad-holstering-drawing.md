## What this is

An **accidental discharge (AD)** while drawing from or returning the pistol to the holster — **DQ** under **10.4.6**.

## RO algorithm

1. **Stop:** call **"STOP!"** the instant the gun fires.
2. **Safety:** the competitor stops moving their hands; if the pistol is back in the holster after the shot, do not allow further manipulation until the **RM** arrives or directs.
3. **Inspect:** check for injuries; locate the impact (often near the feet); have the competitor unload under **RO** control or extract the pistol from the holster only with RO guidance; record that the shot occurred during the holstering / drawing phase.
4. **Document:** **RM** declares **DQ** per **10.4.6**.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Trigger finger:** entering the trigger guard too early during holstering is the most common cause.
* **Inside the holster:** any shot that fires while the pistol is inside the holster is an unconditional **DQ** under **10.4.6**.
* **Recoil reaction:** keep safety control after the shot — do not relax the line.

## Local (FPSU)

Chapter XI §4. Holster type is part of the inspection; unsafe designs may be banned by match rules.
