## What this is

A live round in the gun, or any handling of ammunition inside a **Safety Area (SA)**; in 2026 logic, **any round inside the SA (including in the hand)** is grounds for **DQ** under **10.5.12**.

## RO algorithm

1. **Stop:** immediate **"STOP!"** when a loaded gun or ammunition is found inside the SA.
2. **Safety:** keep the situation under control; **muzzle** to a safe sector of the SA (berm / unloading station); the shooter does not continue manipulation.
3. **Unload:** under RO supervision — unload the gun, **chamber check**, remove the magazine; confirm empty channels.
4. **Document:** call the **RM**, issue **DQ** under **10.5.12**, log it in the match record.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Muzzle:** during the unload, do not relax control of the **muzzle** direction.
* **Manipulations:** only ammunition-free actions are allowed in an SA; combining a firearm with ammunition inside the SA is treated as a **10.5.12** violation.

## Local (FPSU)

Chapter XI §5. Handling ammunition inside a **Safety Area** is among the strictest discipline violations.

## Common RO mistakes

* Ignoring "dry-fire" while a shooter inside the SA is holding a loaded magazine or rounds in hand near the gun.
