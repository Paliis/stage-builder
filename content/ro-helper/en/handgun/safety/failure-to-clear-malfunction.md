## What this is

While clearing a malfunction the shooter manipulates the gun without **muzzle control**: finger inside the trigger guard, chaotic **muzzle** direction, risk of pointing at themselves or bystanders. This is unsafe handling, **DQ** under **10.5.1** (without invoking other sub-rules in this card).

## RO algorithm

1. **Stop:** **"STOP!"** the moment you see an unsafe manipulation while wrestling with a stoppage.
2. **Safety:** the shooter stops the "yanking"; you set the safe **muzzle** direction; finger discipline.
3. **Unload:** if needed, you assist or direct the safe unloading of the jammed gun; no improvisation by the shooter.
4. **Document:** **RM** — **DQ** per **10.5.1**; briefly describe what was unsafe (trigger guard, **recoil reaction** of the shoulder, loss of **muzzle** control).

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* Stress during a stoppage does not justify a finger inside the guard or "waving" the **muzzle**.
* After **STOP**, do not allow continued mag-stripping without supervision.

## Local (FPSU)

Chapter XI §5. A stoppage does not waive the safe-handling requirement; record the episode for the **RM** immediately after a safe unload.
