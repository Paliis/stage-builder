## What this is

A fall, trip or loss of balance during a COF that forces the **RO** to issue a **STOP command** under **8.3.2**. The follow-up depends on whether the episode was an **outside interference** under **8.6.4** or a stop driven purely by safety considerations (2026 logic: technical / interference → **reshoot**; safety without interference → sanctions through the **RM**).

## RO algorithm

1. **Stop:** **STOP command** as soon as continuing would risk injury or losing control of the gun is imminent.
2. **Safety:** do not allow the shooter to "catch up" with the stage; control the **muzzle** direction and finger off the trigger; unload in a safe position under **RO** supervision.
3. **Assessment (RM):** the **RM** decides whether there was **outside interference** (**8.6.4**) or whether the stop was due solely to the shooter's state / unsafe angles after the fall (**8.3.2**).
4. **Decision (Reshoot/DQ):** if substantial **outside interference** is confirmed — mandatory **reshoot** under **8.6.4**; if the cause was safety without interference — the **RM** issues a **DQ** or other sanctions per the match rules (without invoking other rules from this card).

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Touching the ground:** a hand or knee touch without losing **muzzle** control or safe angles may not require **STOP** — assess risk dynamically.
* **Engagement:** any shot fired after a **STOP command** without **RO** authorisation goes immediately to the **RM**.

## Local (FPSU)

Chapter XI §5. A drop of the gun together with the shooter is normally treated as a gross violation — record the facts for the **RM**.
