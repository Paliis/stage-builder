## What this is

An **accidental discharge** into the ground or stage equipment **less than 3 metres** from the competitor (measured per the match procedure) — **DQ** under **10.4.2**. **Exception:** a shot taken at a **scoring target** / a permitted engagement closer than 3 m (2026 logic).

## RO algorithm

1. **Stop:** **"STOP!"** immediately after a shot near the competitor.
2. **Safety:** lock the gun in a safe direction; spectators and helpers stay out of the muzzle line.
3. **Inspect:** measure the distance from the shooter's position to the impact mark; determine whether the round went into a target or into the ground / props; unload under control.
4. **Document:** if confirmed as an AD < 3 m and *not* into a target — **DQ** per **10.4.2**; the **RM** records the measurements and witnesses.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Targets:** a deliberate engagement of a target inside the WSB closer than 3 m is *not* an **10.4.2**; ricochet doubts are resolved by the **RM** against the match's primary rules.
* **Self-injury risk:** a shot into the shooter's own foot or right next to a prop is the textbook **10.4.2** scenario.

## Local (FPSU)

Chapter XI §4. Distance measurement is agreed with the **RM** immediately after the incident.
