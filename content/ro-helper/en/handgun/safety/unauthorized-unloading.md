## What this is

Starting to unload (dropping the magazine, working the slide, holstering a loaded gun) without the corresponding **RO** command after the string ends — unauthorised manipulation, **DQ** under **10.5.1**.

## RO algorithm

1. **Stop:** **"STOP!"** if the shooter begins unloading on their own.
2. **Safety:** **muzzle control**; no chaotic motions; the shooter stops manipulating.
3. **Unload:** continue only on your commands, under full supervision, to a safe state.
4. **Document:** **RM** — **DQ** per **10.5.1**; explain that unloading must follow the standard sequence ("If you are finished… unload…" per the match procedure).

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* Even an experienced shooter does not get an "autopilot" without a command — the **RO** must control the chamber.
* The point is to avoid uncontrolled discharges and ambiguous gun states during the procedure.

## Common RO mistakes

* Tolerating a shooter's "habit" of skipping the verbal command sequence.
