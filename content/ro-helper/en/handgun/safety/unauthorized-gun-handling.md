## What this is

Removing the gun from the holster, case or bag **outside a Safety Area (SA)** and **outside a COF** without an RO command — manipulations that fall under **10.5.1** (**DQ**). Exception — only those actions explicitly permitted inside an **SA** or on a direct instruction from a match official.

## RO algorithm

1. **Stop:** **"STOP!"** the moment a firearm appears in the shooter's hand outside the permitted location / without a command.
2. **Safety:** **muzzle** to a safe direction; bystanders out of the **muzzle line**; no further manipulation until RO instruction.
3. **Unload:** if the gun could be loaded — unload under control; **chamber check**; return to the case / holster only on command.
4. **Document:** **RM** — **DQ** per **10.5.1**; record time and witnesses.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **2026 logic:** any "holster adjustment" in the waiting area that ends in drawing or showing the gun without a command is treated as a **10.5.1** violation.
* **Trigger:** until the situation is fully controlled, finger off the **trigger**.

## Local (FPSU)

Chapter XI §5. Touching the gun outside the procedure is a textbook ground for a **DQ**.
