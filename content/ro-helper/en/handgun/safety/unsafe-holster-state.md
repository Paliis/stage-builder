## What this is

Holstering the pistol in a configuration that creates a risk of an uncontrolled discharge or an unsafe **muzzle** direction (in particular a single-action pistol with the hammer cocked but without the proper **manual safety** / lock when the firearm type and match rules require it) — **DQ** under **10.5.1** and/or **10.5.2** depending on the factual qualification.

## RO algorithm

1. **Stop:** **"STOP!"** when an unsafe state in the holster is detected, or during the holstering itself.
2. **Safety:** do not let the shooter "fix" the state inside the holster; control the hands and the **muzzle** after a careful extraction per the **RM**'s procedure.
3. **Unload:** slow extraction under RO command; unload; **chamber check**; finger off the **trigger**.
4. **Document:** **RM** issues **DQ** referencing **10.5.1** / **10.5.2** depending on the specific qualification of the episode.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Gun type:** exposed-hammer pistols need extra attention to the hammer state and the **manual safety** before holstering.
* **Recoil reaction:** after the draw, do not relax finger discipline on the **trigger**.

## Common RO mistakes

* Letting the shooter engage the **manual safety** *after* the unsafe state has formed in the holster, instead of calling **STOP** and a controlled extraction.
