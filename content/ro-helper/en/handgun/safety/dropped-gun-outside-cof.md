## What this is

A **dropped gun** anywhere on the venue outside a course of fire (parking, waiting area, vendor row, etc.) — **DQ** under **10.5.14** (the gun is outside the permitted COF / **SA** context).

## RO algorithm

1. **Stop:** **"STOP!"**; get the attention of everyone in the area; halt movement around the **dropped gun**.
2. **Isolate:** establish a perimeter around the impact spot; the shooter **does not** approach the gun on their own; clear the crowd from any possible **muzzle** line.
3. **Safe pickup:** only an authorised person (**RO** / **RM**) lifts the pistol; **unload**; **chamber check**; **muzzle** to the berm during the manipulation.
4. **Document:** **RM** issues **DQ** per **10.5.14**; incident report to the **MD**.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* If the shooter touches the **dropped gun** themselves outside a COF — that is an additional risk factor; record the timeline for the **RM**.
* Inside an **SA**, the primary rules govern manipulations — a drop there is not "automatically harmless"; pickup and **unload** follow the **SA** procedure.

## Local (FPSU)

Chapter XI §5. A drop in the parking area is a textbook ground for immediate match removal under **10.5.14**.
