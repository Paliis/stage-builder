## What this is

In a fixed-time stage, the shooter exceeds the WSB shot limit. An **extra shot** is penalised under **9.4.5** (2026 logic: shots and extra hits are penalised separately).

## RO algorithm

1. **Observation:** count every actual shot during the time window (sound, recoil, cycle); a "dry" shot during Fixed Time counts as an **extra shot** if it falls inside the stage.
2. **Assessment:** compare with the WSB limit; isolate any shot fired after the time-end signal — a separate episode for the **RM** / **time penalty** regulation.
3. **Penalty:** one procedural per **extra shot** under **9.4.5**; do not merge with an **extra hit** on paper (separate card).
4. **Document:** announce the totals to the shooter and record the procedurals on the scoresheet.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Miss:** an **extra shot** is penalised even without a hit.
* **After signal:** an **extra shot** plus a shot after the **time penalty** ends are normally two separate episodes for **9.4.5** / match regulations (the **RM** rules).
* **Recoil reaction:** do not confuse a misfire with a non-firing reload — a misfire without a shot is normally not counted as a unit of fire under **9.4.5**.

## Common RO mistakes

* Ignoring a "round dropped to the ground" as "not a shot" in Fixed Time.
