## What this is

A hit on a No-Shoot during the **engagement**. Sanctions under **9.4.4**: typically **−10** points for a full calibre hole on the No-Shoot (verify match format and primary rules).

## RO algorithm

1. **Observation:** record sound / visual evidence of a likely No-Shoot hit during the stage.
2. **Assessment:** after the stage, inspect the No-Shoot for full bullet holes; usually limited to no more than two scoring penalties on a single No-Shoot under **9.4.4** (verify the match's primary rules).
3. **Penalty:** under **9.4.4** — either a **time penalty** (fixed time / matching format) or **−10** points for a full hole in classic point scoring (verify primary rules); a pass-through hit through the No-Shoot into a scoring target — record both the penalty and the scoring hit per the match scoring procedure.
4. **Document:** record in the No-Shoot column / corresponding ESS field before the target is pasted.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Edge:** if there is no full calibre hole within the No-Shoot silhouette — the **9.4.4** penalty is usually not assessed.
* **Recoil reaction:** control transitions between targets to reduce the risk of clipping a No-Shoot.

## Common RO mistakes

* Counting more than two penalties on one No-Shoot when the rules cap the maximum.
