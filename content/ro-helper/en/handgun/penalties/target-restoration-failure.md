## What this is

Before the shooter starts, the stage is not in its initial state: targets are not raised, activators not reset, there is premature **pasting** (**9.1.3**) or traces of a previous **engagement** without **restoration** (**9.1.4**). During a COF this distorts the result — stop and the **RM**'s **reshoot** decision.

## RO algorithm

1. **Inspect:** before the start and during the stage check the position of moving targets, poppers, and paper; spot any "invisible" or inactive **scoring target**.
2. **Assess:** qualify as a stage-readiness violation under **9.1.4** (not restored from the previous run) or under **9.1.3** if the target's state precludes a fair score.
3. **Announce:** issue the stop command per match procedure; unload under control; report to the **RM**.
4. **Document:** the **RM** orders a **reshoot** after a full stage **restoration**; do not apply FTE for a target that was not in its working state through the venue's fault.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Order:** fix the cause first, then **reshoot**; do not score the spoiled attempt.
* **Muzzle:** during the stop, **muzzle** direction safety has priority.

## Common RO mistakes

* Scoring misses / FTE on a shooter for a target the helpers failed to raise before the start.
