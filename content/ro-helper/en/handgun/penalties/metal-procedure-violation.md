## What this is

**Engagement** of a steel target in breach of the WSB (wrong position / port, wrong activation order) or an attempt to physically "support" the steel to keep it from falling. Procedural sanction — **10.2.2**; gross holding of the target — **RM** for a discipline decision (no other rule references in this card).

## RO algorithm

1. **Observation:** record the **muzzle** line, foot position, and the moment of impact on the popper / plate against the WSB.
2. **Assessment:** decide whether the target fell from a permitted **engagement**; whether the shooter tried to support the steel with hand / body.
3. **Penalty:** one procedural under **10.2.2** per target engaged with a procedural violation; activation of dependent targets on an invalid engagement — 0 points on the chain if the briefing sets that.
4. **Document:** record on the scoresheet; for steel-supporting — call the **RM** at once.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Activation:** if the steel was meant to release a moving target but was hit with a procedural breach, scoring of the activated target is decided by the WSB and the **RM**.
* **Recoil reaction:** do not use the body to dampen a falling plate — that does not replace the procedural penalty.

## Common RO mistakes

* Charging a procedural per shot at the steel instead of one violation per target on the procedural error.
