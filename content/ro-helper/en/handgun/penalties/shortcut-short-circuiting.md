## What this is

The shooter did not follow the mandatory stage path, which let them shorten the distance or time. Sanction — procedural penalty.

## RO algorithm

1. **Observation:** record the moment the shooter bypasses a mandatory stage element or steps over a fault line to shorten the path.
2. **Assessment:** decide whether a meaningful advantage was gained (time saved or simplified target engagement).
3. **Penalty:** 1 procedural per case or 1 per shot fired after the shortcut (**10.2.2**).
4. **Document:** enter the penalty count on the scoresheet.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Fault line:** if the shooter crossed the line but did not shoot and stepped back — only 1 procedural per case.
* **Advantage:** if the shortcut allowed engaging a target unreachable on the proper path — strict per-shot sanctions.

## Local (FPSU)

Chapter XI §2. FPSU pays special attention to clear prop construction to rule out unintentional shortcuts.

## Common RO mistakes

* Per-shot procedurals without proven significant advantage.
