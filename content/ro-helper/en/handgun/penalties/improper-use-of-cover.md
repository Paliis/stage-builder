## What this is

**Engaging** targets without proper use of **cover** when the WSB explicitly requires shooting from / behind cover. Sanction — procedural penalty under **10.2.8**.

## RO algorithm

1. **Observation:** record the shooter's foot and body position relative to the **cover** edge during the **engagement** of the relevant targets.
2. **Assessment:** decide whether a substantial part of the body extends past the line the WSB defines as the **cover** boundary (often a fault line).
3. **Penalty:** one procedural per target engaged in breach of the **cover** requirement (**10.2.8**), not per individual shot.
4. **Document:** enter the procedurals on the scoresheet and a brief note for any appeal.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **WSB:** the penalty applies only if **cover** use is mandatory in the briefing.
* **Line:** stepping past a marked **cover** boundary is normally treated as a violation for that target.

## Common RO mistakes

* Counting a procedural per shot instead of per target (**10.2.8**).
