## What this is

**Engagement** of targets in an order that contradicts a hard WSB requirement (e.g. "T1 first, then T2"). Sanction — procedural penalty under **10.2.2**.

## RO algorithm

1. **Observation:** record the engagement order across target groups during the **engagement**.
2. **Assessment:** identify the first moment the WSB-defined order is broken; if the shooter corrects before the next target — do not duplicate the procedural for that target.
3. **Penalty:** one procedural per target engaged "out of order" (**10.2.2**).
4. **Document:** record the number of procedurals on the scoresheet.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Freestyle:** the penalty applies only when the WSB explicitly fixes the order; an arbitrary "logical" order absent from the WSB text is not penalised.
* **Self-correction:** before firing on the next target after realising the mistake — no procedural for that episode.

## Common RO mistakes

* Calling a procedural for an "inconvenient" order that the WSB never forbade.
