## What this is

Engaging targets from a position not foreseen by the briefing for those targets. Sanction — procedural penalty per shot.

## RO algorithm

1. **Observation:** record the moment the shooter starts engaging targets from the wrong port or position.
2. **Assessment:** check the WSB requirement on permitted engagement zones for the specific targets.
3. **Penalty:** 1 procedural per shot fired from the wrong location (**10.2.2**).
4. **Document:** clear record of shot count and target numbers on the scoresheet.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Principle:** if the stage is "Freestyle", the shooter chooses the position, but if the WSB carries a hard instruction ("Target 1 from Window A") — it is mandatory.
* **Fault line:** do not confuse with a regular fault line; here the stage's tactical condition is the violation.

## Common RO mistakes

* Calling a penalty for shooting from a different position when the WSB only listed a "recommended" rather than mandatory position.
