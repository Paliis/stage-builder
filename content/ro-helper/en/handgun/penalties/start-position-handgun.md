## What this is

Failure to assume the **start position** as fixed in the WSB at the moment of the start signal — a violation of **8.2.2**.

## RO algorithm

1. **Observation:** before "Attention" verify hands, body, contact with firearm / equipment per WSB.
2. **Classification:** **Creeping** — body movement / posture change after "Attention" but before the signal. **Wrong start position** — hands / body not where the WSB describes from the very beginning (2026 logic).
3. **Penalty:** after the COF — one procedural error for the **8.2.2** violation (processing per match protocol / scoring).
4. **Document:** record on the scoresheet: what exactly differed from the WSB and whether **creeping** was involved.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Relaxed at sides:** arms relaxed alongside the body; palms toward the body; no contact with firearm or pouches if the WSB so requires.
* **Surrender:** elbows raised, palms visible to the front — as in the briefing.
* **Muzzle control:** at the start, the pistol stays in the holster until "Load and Make Ready" unless described otherwise.

## Common RO mistakes

* Starting with fingers on the belt / pouches when the WSB requires fully free hands.
