## What this is

Insulting language or profanity directed at match officials — **unsporting conduct** under **10.6.1**; 2026 logic: **automatic DQ** (processed under **10.3** after **RM** confirmation).

## RO algorithm

1. **Observation:** quote verbatim or write down the phrase, language, and context (during the COF or after).
2. **Warning/Stop:** if on the line — "Stop"; halt firearm manipulation; **muzzle** safe.
3. **RM call:** the **RM** interviews witnesses; confirms the **DQ** under **10.6.1**; applies **10.3**.
4. **Document:** record the **DQ** on the scoresheet with the **10.6.1** reference; materials for the report and the **arbitration fee** per the regulations.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* Offensive gestures are equivalent to verbal abuse under **10.6.1** at the **RM**'s discretion.
* Do not enter a verbal exchange — record only and escalate.

## Local (FPSU)

Chapter XI. The federation ethics code on public respect — additional to **10.6.1**.
