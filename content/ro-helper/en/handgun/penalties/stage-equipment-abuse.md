## What this is

**Abuse of equipment** under **10.6.1**: deliberate kicking, striking with hand or firearm of targets, props, timers, or other equipment; **unsporting conduct** that damages the venue. Typically — **DQ**.

## RO algorithm

1. **Stop:** immediate **STOP command** the moment a deliberate strike / **engagement** of non-target items with intent is detected.
2. **Safety:** prevent further actions by the shooter; **muzzle** in a safe sector; unload only under **RO** control after movement stops.
3. **Assessment (RM):** pass the description, witnesses, photos / damage inspection to the **RM**; distinguish deliberate **abuse** from accidental contact during a fall (**outside interference** / **REF** is decided by the **RM** separately).
4. **Decision (Reshoot/DQ):** under **10.6.1** with confirmed **abuse** — **DQ**; a **reshoot** is not used as "compensation" instead of an **RM** disciplinary decision.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Unintentional contact:** brushing a prop in a fall without a "rage" strike may be a technical **equipment failure** or **outside interference** — not automatically **10.6.1**.
* **Recoil reaction:** the risk of mount tear-off after strikes — extra venue check before resuming shooting.

## Common RO mistakes

* Charging only a procedural instead of immediately involving the **RM** for a **10.6.1** DQ.
