## What this is

**Unsporting conduct** that discredits the sport: aggression, profanity, demonstrative gear abuse, deceiving officials — under **10.6**; **disqualification (DQ)** processed under **10.3**.

## RO algorithm

1. **Observation:** record facts, words, gestures; identify witnesses.
2. **Warning/Stop:** if the event happens during the COF — "Stop"; firearm into a safe state (**muzzle** safe, fingers off the trigger).
3. **RM call:** 2026 logic: profanity directed at officials — **automatic DQ** under **10.6.1**; other **unsporting conduct** events — decided by the **RM** under **10.6** / **10.3**.
4. **Document:** report to the **RM** / **MD**; record the **DQ** in scoring; preserve data for a possible **arbitration fee**.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* Profanity may be heard by spectators even without a direct address — the **10.6.1** test is for the **RM**.
* A **10.6.1** DQ normally does not "return" the shooter to the match; only the underlying ground is appealable per match procedure.
