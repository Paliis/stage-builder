## What this is

A range **equipment failure** (targets, poppers, activators, props) that affects fair **engagement**. This is not the shooter's fault; the decision is a **reshoot** under **4.6.1**.

## RO algorithm

1. **Stop:** **"STOP!"** if the **equipment failure** prevents a correct COF (e.g. a popper does not fall on a clean hit).
2. **Safety:** standard unload under RO control.
3. **Repair:** notify the **RM** / **MD**; remove or isolate the malfunction before resuming.
4. **Document:** issue a mandatory **reshoot**; do not score the time and points of the attempt with the **equipment failure**.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Popper:** a faulty mechanism or a jam — **REF**, not a shooter miss.
* **Props:** a stand collapse, a torn activator cable — typical grounds for a **reshoot** (**4.6.1**).

## Common RO mistakes

* Forcing the shooter to continue when the result is spoiled by an **equipment failure**.
