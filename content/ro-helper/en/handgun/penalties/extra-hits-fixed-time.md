## What this is

A scoring target shows more bullet holes than the Fixed Time procedure permits. The **extra hit** is penalised under **9.4.5** (alongside an **extra shot** count if the **RO** logged both).

## RO algorithm

1. **Observation:** after the stage, inspect the paper targets before pasting; mark every full hole.
2. **Assessment:** compare the hit count with the WSB per-target limit; identify the surplus; on doubt — **RM**.
3. **Penalty:** procedural under **9.4.5** per **extra hit**; for points distribution, the highest-value zones are removed if the match scoring procedure provides for that in Fixed Time.
4. **Document:** enter procedurals and the final zones on the scoresheet before the target is pasted.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Double counting:** a procedural for an **extra shot** and one for an **extra hit** under **9.4.5** are both possible if both episodes are confirmed.
* **Muzzle:** uncontrolled bursts increase the risk of **extra hits** — record them for the **RM**'s analysis.

## Common RO mistakes

* Counting all holes without applying **9.4.5** to the surplus.
