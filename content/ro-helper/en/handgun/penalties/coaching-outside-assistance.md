## What this is

The shooter receives a **coaching cue** or other **outside assistance** (verbal, gesture) during the COF under **8.6**.

## RO algorithm

1. **Observation:** record the source and timing of the **coaching cue**; whether the shooter responded and whether their behaviour changed in their favour.
2. **Warning/Stop:** warn the offender; if needed, "Stop" and halt the COF when continuation is unsafe or the result is already distorted.
3. **RM call:** 2026 logic: if the shooter **acted on** the assistance — procedural sanction under **8.6** and the **RM** decision; repetition or deliberate organisation of **coaching cues** — escalation to **DQ** under **10.6** / **10.3** (the **RM** processes).
4. **Document:** record on the scoresheet, brief incident report (who, what was said / done); per match policy — prepare materials for the **arbitration fee** / appeal.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **RO** safety commands are not **outside assistance**.
* Match level sets the strictness; at high levels, a hint from the stands is almost always sanctioned.

## Common RO mistakes

* DQ'ing a shooter without proof of deliberate use of a **coaching cue** when an **8.6** sanction is sufficient.
