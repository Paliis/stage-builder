## What this is

Safety violations during a **shoot-off**, where the risk of losing **muzzle** control and managing fire tempo is elevated.

## RO algorithm

1. **Verification:** two ROs / observation positions — control of both shooters' **muzzles**, especially during reloads and half-turns.
2. **Assessment:** check that **engagement** directions and movement do not create cross-fire risks between the lanes.
3. **Requirements:** if one competitor is DQ'd, command **"STOP!"** for **both**; the **shoot-off** does not continue until safe unloading is done.
4. **Document:** report the incident through the RM; Section 10 sanctions apply per the match rules (**muzzle** direction, cross-fire, etc.).

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Cross-fire:** any deliberate or gross pointing of the **muzzle** at the opponent's lane is qualified by the RM under Section 10 safety rules.
* **Long gun / PCC:** if the **shoot-off** uses long guns under the regulations — the in-motion safety requirements are as strict as in the main match (coordinate with the RM).

## Local (FPSU)

Chapter XI. A DQ during a shoot-off is recorded separately; the impact on the main-match standing is set by MD regulations.

## Common RO mistakes

* Stopping only one shooter while the other continues to move with the gun after **"STOP!"**.
