## What this is

Influence on the COF by people who are not the shooter and are not performing **RO** duties — under **8.6** (including **8.6.2**, **8.6.4**).

## RO algorithm

1. **Observation:** what exactly happened (physical contact, entering the **engagement** zone, voice **coaching**, an animal on the line).
2. **Warning/Stop:** "Stop" if there is danger or score distortion; remove the third party from the **muzzle** trajectory.
3. **RM call:** **reshoot** under **8.6.4** if the interference was accidental and impeded the shooter; deliberate **outside assistance** — sanctions under **8.6.2** and possible escalation to a **DQ** under **10.6** / **10.3**.
4. **Document:** record the event, witnesses; remove the offender from the COF area on the **RM** / **MD** instruction.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* A photographer who bumps the shooter — the textbook **8.6.4** scenario (reshoot by **RM** decision).
* Spectators too close to the line — prevention before the start, not "treatment" mid-COF.

## Common RO mistakes

* Continuing the stage after a clear interference without "Stop" and an **RM** call.
