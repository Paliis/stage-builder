## What this is

The **RO** operates inside the safe practicable zone of the stage and maintains conditions per **2.1.5**: safe escort paths, stable surface, no extraneous obstacles next to the shooter's movement line.

## RO algorithm

1. **Assessment:** before the string, identify the shooter's path, casing-ejection zones, points where the **muzzle** can swing; pick a position with visibility on finger and slide without crossing the **engagement** line.
2. **Action:** keep a distance that lets you react to **STOP** without disrupting movement; avoid slippery patches or clean them per **stage restoration** procedure.
3. **Communicate:** wear **active ear protection** to hear shots and **RM** commands; on collision risk with the shooter — call **STOP** immediately and escalate to the **RM**.
4. **Document:** for a stop due to a danger to the **RO** — log the event; the **RM** may grant a **reshoot** for the shooter.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* The shooter's **muzzle control** must be in your field of view; do not escort too tightly to avoid accidental contact during **recoil reaction**.
* Wear **eye protection** and hearing protection just as the regulations require for line staff.

## Common RO mistakes

* Standing in the possible contact zone with the shooter during movement or a fall on a damaged surface.
