## What this is

A consistent sequence after a **STOP command** under **8.3.2**: safety, **RM** report, decision on a **reshoot** (**8.6.4**) or a **DQ** (in particular **unsporting conduct** / a shot fired after **STOP** — **10.6.1** as qualified by the **RM**).

## RO algorithm

1. **Stop:** loud, clear **STOP command**; stop the timer per the match procedure if possible.
2. **Safety:** the shooter stays in a safe position; **muzzle** to the berm; unload and **chamber check** only on **RO** instruction.
3. **Assessment (RM):** the **RO** reports to the **RM**: cause — danger, **RO** technical error, **outside interference**, or shooter **unsporting conduct**.
4. **Decision (Reshoot/DQ):** an erroneous stop or **outside interference** — **reshoot** under **8.6.4**; a stop driven by confirmed danger / **10.6.1** — **DQ** or other **RM** decisions.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Shot after STOP:** treat as a candidate for **10.6.1** after questioning the shooter and witnesses.
* **Reshoot:** do not confuse with a "restart" without an **RM** decision.

## Local (FPSU)

Chapter VIII §3. The **STOP command** must be audible even through active ear protection — add visual signals when needed.
