## What this is

An error in deciding the **shoot-off** winner due to a misread of the **stop plate** falling or a missed scoring target.

## RO algorithm

1. **Verification:** confirm that all WSB scoring targets are hit before the **stop plate** falls (if the order is set in the briefing).
2. **Assessment:** if the **stop plate** falls but a scoring target is still un-engaged — the run is lost per the **shoot-off** rules and **1.2.2**.
3. **Requirements:** if there is doubt over a fall (wind / ricochet / glitch) — do not declare a winner; call the RM for a reshoot or a match-procedure decision.
4. **Document:** record the winner in the bracket and the run time/order per the regulations.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Order:** any mandatory engagement order from the WSB — breaking it loses the **shoot-off** even if the **stop plate** falls.
* **Simultaneous fall:** re-run or tie-breaker per **1.2.2** and the MD briefing.

## Local (FPSU)

Recording on the **electronic scoresheet** or paper — duplicate per Stats requirements.

## Common RO mistakes

* Declaring the winner only on the last target's fall, without verifying the full hit set.
