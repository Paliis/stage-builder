## What this is

Hosting honoured guests without interfering with judging or **engagement** safety — owned by the **MD** and **7.1** officials; at large matches there are extra expectations under **6.1.2**.

## RO algorithm

1. **Standard: preparation:** before entering the bay — a mini-briefing: 180°, fingers, PPE, silence during the COF; movement only with an authorised **MD** escort.
2. **Standard: execution:** keep guests in the dedicated viewing area; no access to the line until "Range is clear"; ensure no one crosses **muzzle** trajectories or the **RO** working positions.
3. **Standard: coordination:** any deviation from plan — stop the group, call an **MD** / safety **7.1** representative.
4. **Standard: documentation:** any guest incident affecting match flow or results is reported to the **RM** for the match record under **9.10** if the primary rules require.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* Priority is the safety of participants and officials, not the "VIP photo" protocol.
* Guest zoning is agreed before the squad starts, not mid-string.
