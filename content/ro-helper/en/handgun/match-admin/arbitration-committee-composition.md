## What this is

Forming the **arbitration committee** to hear an **appeal** under **11.2** of the Jan 2026 primary rules.

## RO algorithm (legal protocol)

1. **Identify:** once the **appeal** is registered, the **RM** convenes the committee per **11.2** (member count, qualifications).
2. **Document:** record member names and any conflict of interest; the stage **RO** directly involved in the incident does not sit on the committee.
3. **Escalate:** the committee hears the shooter (statement only, no participation in deliberation), reviews evidence; the majority decision is logged in the report.
4. **Record decision:** announce the verdict to the shooter and the **MD**; update the **official scoreboard**; the **11.2** decision is final within the primary rules.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* Decision deadlines are tied to the close of scoring windows unless the **MD** specifies otherwise.
* **Shoot-off** and other special formats do not bypass **11.2** if a procedural **appeal** is filed.

## Local (FPSU)

Chapter XI. Regional representatives may sit on the committee per the national federation regulations.
