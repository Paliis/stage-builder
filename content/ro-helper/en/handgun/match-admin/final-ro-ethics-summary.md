## What this is

The official's compass: **IROA ethics** (honesty, neutrality, service to the competition) combined with **10.5**, crisp **8.3.1** commands, and a clean **9.10** record.

## RO algorithm

1. **Honesty:** decisions for the rules and the shooter, not for the crew's convenience; in doubt — **RM**, never on your own.
2. **Commands:** **8.3.1** standard; calm under **recoil reaction** stress and **echo** noise.
3. **Respect:** firm on **10.5**; courteous in communication; conflict — escalation, not duel.
4. **Safety:** **10.5** without exception; **muzzle**, **engagement** zones, **timer** and the **checklist** — daily discipline; **9.10** reflects the fact, not the desired outcome.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* Diligentia, Vis, Celeritas — apply equally to officials' discipline and the speed of correct decisions.
* **IROA ethics** forbid hints and double standards between shooters.

## Local (FPSU)

The official sustains a culture of safety and respect in the community; follows the federation **MD** code.
