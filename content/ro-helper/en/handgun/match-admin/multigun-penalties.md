## What this is

Procedural violations specific to **multigun**, falling under **Appendix G** and Section 10.

## RO algorithm

1. **Verification:** record the **engagement** of a target with a non-permitted firearm or stepping outside a transition without WSB authorisation.
2. **Assessment:** determine whether a meaningful advantage was gained (firearm order, missed targets) per WSB and **Appendix G**.
3. **Requirements:** apply procedural penalties per shot/episode according to the match procedure and coordination with the RM for disputed FTEs.
4. **Document:** enter the totals into the **electronic scoresheet** or paper record immediately after the stage.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **FTE / skipped target:** skipping a target group because the next firearm was not ready is scored per WSB and match rules; do not double up sanctions without a basis in the briefing.
* **Wrong firearm:** the typical consequence is a procedural per shot or per episode — clarify with the RM for the specific format.

## Local (FPSU)

Coordinate with Stats and the RM before finalising non-standard mixed stages.

## Common RO mistakes

* Stacking several penalty classes on a single episode without separating the grounds.
