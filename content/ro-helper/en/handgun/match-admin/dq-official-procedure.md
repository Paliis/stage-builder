## What this is

Correctly issuing a **disqualification (DQ)** under **10.3** (including grounds in **10.6**) while minimising disputes and locking down clean documentation.

## RO algorithm

1. **Observation:** the **DQ** ground (rule, fact, witnesses) is formulated before approaching the **RM**.
2. **Warning/Stop:** stop the shooter; ensure a safe gun state (unload per the **RM** protocol); halt the **engagement**.
3. **RM call:** the **RM** confirms the **DQ**, verifies the rule citation (**10.3**, optionally **10.6.x**); informs the shooter clearly.
4. **Document:** record on the scoresheet (time, **10.3** / **10.6** rule); **RO** / **RM** signatures per match policy; explain the **arbitration fee** and appeal deadlines per the **MD**'s primary rules.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* A **DQ** normally voids the entire match for the shooter — per **10.3** and the primary rules.
* An incorrect rule number on the record complicates the appeal — double-check with the **RM**.

## Common RO mistakes

* Pulling accreditation without the **RM** / stats office.
* Announcing the **DQ** without calmly informing the shooter of the **10.3** ground.
