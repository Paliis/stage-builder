## What this is

The professional minimum of **7.1** at international matches is reinforced by the **IROA protocol**: discipline, commands, and the federation's image.

## RO algorithm

1. **Standard: preparation:** arrive by the time defined by the **MD**/CRO; verify the timer, tablet, radio; uniform and gear per primary rules and the **IROA protocol**.
2. **Standard: execution:** standard commands only; no personal conversation with shooters during a COF; keep distance from the **muzzle** trajectory and **engagement** zones; stay calm during protests.
3. **Standard: coordination:** the **RO** does not handle disputes alone — escalate to the **RM** under **7.1** at once; at **Level IV–V** (**6.1.2**) conduct expectations are higher.
4. **Standard: documentation:** any decision affecting the score is reflected in scoring per **9.10** and Stats policy.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* The **RO** represents the rule system, not personal opinion; scoring-zone doubts are resolved per the **MD** primary rules — there is no "automatic shooter benefit" replacing the rules.
* After a stage swap or a long shift — a brief gear self-audit (see the self-audit card).
