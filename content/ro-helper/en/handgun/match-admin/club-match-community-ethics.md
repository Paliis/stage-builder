## What this is

Sustaining respect, mutual help, and fairness at a **club match**; halting unsporting behaviour under **10.6.1**.

## RO algorithm

1. **Organisation:** involve participants in **stage restoration** (targets, casings) outside the shooter currently preparing for a string; spread the load fairly.
2. **Control:** stop bullying, public mocking of results, sabotage of line work; on repetition, escalate to the **RM** / **MD** for a decision under **10.6.1**.
3. **Reporting:** record feedback on stage safety and equipment status; warn the organisers of any risk before the match continues.
4. **Document:** before closing the bay, verify all scoresheets and signatures; document **10.6.1** incidents in the report.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* The **RO** sets the tone: calm communication during the **engagement** and when explaining decisions reduces conflicts.
* Post-match results discussions are welcome as long as they do not turn into pressure on the **RO** or shooters.
