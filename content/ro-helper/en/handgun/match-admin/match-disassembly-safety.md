## What this is

Safe stage teardown after **7.1** officials have confirmed the **9.10** scoring procedures are complete for the relevant data; the teardown **protocol** is set by the **MD**.

## RO algorithm

1. **Standard: preparation:** obtain **MD**/CRO authorisation to start teardown; verify there is no active **engagement** on the stage; firearm-change zones / SA — per plan, usually dismantled last.
2. **Standard: execution:** visually search for rounds / equipment in COF zones and behind props; careful disassembly so the **muzzle** / metal parts do not point at people.
3. **Standard: coordination:** damage to steel targets, loss of paper with personal data — report to **MD** **7.1**; paper destruction — per **MD** confidentiality policy.
4. **Standard: documentation:** inventory and storage transfer record; confirm critical data has been pulled to Stats before stage records are deleted from the **9.10** system.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* Do not leave loaded magazines or rounds "for helpers" on a former COF.
* Environmental concerns and casing collection — per local range rules and the **MD**.

## Common RO mistakes

* Tearing down a stage before agreeing with Stats while **9.10** corrections are still possible.
