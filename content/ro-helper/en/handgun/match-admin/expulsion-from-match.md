## What this is

Last-resort measure: revoking accreditation and removing a person from the venue for a gross conduct violation — **10.6.2** in connection with **10.6** and the consequences of **10.3**.

## RO algorithm

1. **Observation:** describe the event, the people involved, the safety risk and the **engagement** context.
2. **Warning/Stop:** keep a safe distance; do not provoke the conflict; ensure firearm work in the area is halted.
3. **RM call:** call the **RM** / **MD** immediately; only the administration declares expulsion and a **DQ** under **10.3** if needed.
4. **Document:** written report, witnesses; pass to stats / region per **MD** policy; mention the **arbitration fee** if the match procedure requires it.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* The decision can extend to associated persons if the match's primary rules say so in the briefing.
* Physical removal is for security staff / authorised **MD** personnel only — not the **RO** acting alone.

## Common RO mistakes

* Announcing expulsion without involving the **MD** / **RM**.
