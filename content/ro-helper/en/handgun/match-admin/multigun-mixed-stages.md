## What this is

A breach of the **multigun** procedure when several firearm types are used on a single stage per **Appendix G** and the WSB.

## RO algorithm

1. **Verification:** at the start, confirm each firearm's **ready state** and placement in the WSB-defined zones.
2. **Assessment:** track the shooter's movement between firing positions and the firearm-type / target-group match.
3. **Requirements:** enforce the firearm change order and the firearm state in the **transition zone** per **Appendix G**; stop on any safety risk.
4. **Document:** procedural consequences per Section 10 and the match procedure; DQ only on RM decision for safety violations.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Transition:** the firearm left in the transition zone must comply with WSB on unloaded state / safety / **muzzle** position — see **Appendix G**.
* **Engagement:** targets are engaged only with the permitted firearm type unless WSB allows freestyle for that group.

## Local (FPSU)

Chapter XIII. Additional national requirements for firearm changes — in the MD briefing.

## Common RO mistakes

* Ignoring the firearm state in the basket / rack while the shooter is engaging from another platform.
