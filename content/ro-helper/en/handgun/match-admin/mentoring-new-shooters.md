## What this is

Supporting beginners at a **club match** within the duties of officials under **7.1.1** and the briefing / stage-admission rules of **8.6.1**, without tactical "coaching" during the COF.

## RO algorithm

1. **Briefing:** explain the commands, **start position**, safe **muzzle** direction and the unloading sequence to the beginner; confirm the **STOP** signals are understood.
2. **Supervision:** pick an escort position with full view of the hands, the **muzzle**, and the trajectory; be ready to call **STOP**, not to "back-stop" with your own gun.
3. **Hints:** at **Level I** brief safety reminders are allowed ("finger", "muzzle direction") without "go where" or "next target" hints; any tactical hint during a string is outside **mentoring** and may be sanctioned per the primary rules.
4. **Debrief:** after "Range is Clear" briefly review procedural and safety errors; **mentoring** after the string does not undo sanctions if violations were already recorded.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Mentoring** ≠ improving someone's time with hints during the **engagement**.
* A gross safety violation (unsafe **muzzle**, AD, etc.) — a **DQ** with no exception for the beginner; explain calmly but record honestly.

## Common RO mistakes

* Ignoring a beginner's unsafe habits "to avoid scaring them" — that undermines **club match** safety culture.
