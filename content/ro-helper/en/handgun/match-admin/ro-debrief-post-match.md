## What this is

A short shift review: **timer** glitches, **8.3.1** command issues, **10.5** incidents, lessons for **9.10** — in the spirit of **IROA ethics** (confidentiality, respect).

## RO algorithm

1. **Cases:** DQs, stops, disputed scoring episodes — facts, no names in public.
2. **Stage safety:** were there **10.5** "traps"; what to change for future stages.
3. **Logistics:** **timer**, **echo**, targets, helpers — what slowed **9.10**.
4. **IROA ethics:** constructive feedback inside the crew; pass decisions to the **RM** / **MD** in writing for process improvement.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* The debrief does not replace the official **MD** disciplinary channel.
* Critique of colleagues — privately; the goal is a safer **engagement** at the next match.

## Common RO mistakes

* Skipping the debrief and repeating the same **timer** / restoration glitches.
