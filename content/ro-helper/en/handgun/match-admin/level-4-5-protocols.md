## What this is

The **6.1.2** match-level requirements (including World Shoot) and the official roles in **7.1** combined with the **IROA protocol**: an identical standard for every team.

## RO algorithm

1. **Standard: preparation:** familiarise yourself with the **MD**'s primary rules, the CRO/RM schedule and the **IROA protocol** expectations; uniform, language of commands, **engagement** zones — exactly as in the briefing.
2. **Standard: execution:** 2026 logic: at **Level IV–V**, the **RO** maintains **absolute neutrality** — no hints, "favourite" shooters, or off-record comments during the COF; commands are crisp, no extra dialogue.
3. **Standard: coordination:** technical stage questions are closed through CRO/RM under **7.1**, not "on the spot" with the participant; rule disputes go through the **RM** channel only.
4. **Standard: documentation:** double-control of stage results per **MD** policy and **9.10** (electronic + paper if the primary rules require).

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* The official working language and public conduct follow the **MD** primary rules and the **IROA protocol**.
* In camera and spectator zones, the participant's **muzzle** and **engagement** trajectories are watched even more strictly; **recoil reaction** does not justify softening the commands.

## Common RO mistakes

* An informal piece of advice to a fellow countryman — a violation of neutrality and the **IROA protocol**, sanctions to the official by **MD/RM** decision.
