## What this is

Refusing to follow an official **RO** instruction on safety or match procedure after a clear request — unsporting conduct, **DQ** under **10.6.1**.

## RO algorithm

1. **Stop:** if needed — **"STOP!"**, halt the COF or the unsafe action; create distance.
2. **Safety:** **muzzle control**; the shooter does not continue firing or manipulation; finger off the trigger.
3. **Unload:** standard unload under supervision after the scenario is stopped.
4. **Document:** call **RM** — **DQ** per **10.6.1**; brief incident record and witnesses.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* Clarifying questions about rules are allowed; refusing to comply with a PPE, boundary, or unload command after a repeated instruction falls under **10.6.1**.
* If the refusal creates immediate danger — **stop** without debate.

## Common RO mistakes

* A long argument instead of recording the fact and calling the **RM**.
