## What this is

Professional development of an official **7.1**: a brief self-review after the match, aligned with **6.1.2** expectations at large matches and the **IROA protocol**.

## RO algorithm

1. **Standard: preparation:** collect a list of disputed episodes, appeals, your own "Stops"; have squad notes and the **MD** primary rules at hand.
2. **Standard: execution:** assess safety: were there command delays, was the distance from the **muzzle** and **engagement** zones correct; were there extra words during the COF.
3. **Standard: coordination:** discuss feedback with the CRO / crew without blame; at international matches — ask the **IROA** mentor if the protocol provides for it.
4. **Standard: documentation:** record findings in your personal officials' diary; identify rule topics for review; pass proposals to the **MD** to improve **9.10** / next-match logistics.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* The self-audit does not replace formal **MD** disciplinary review, but it cuts repeat-error risk.
* After multi-day matches — recover sleep / load; burnout degrades the quality of **engagement** judging.
