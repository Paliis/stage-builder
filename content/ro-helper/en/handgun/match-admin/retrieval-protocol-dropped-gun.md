## What this is

A single safe sequence for regaining control of a **dropped gun** after events under **10.5.3** or **10.5.14**. The shooter is **strictly forbidden** to pick the gun up themselves.

## RO algorithm

1. **Stop:** **"STOP!"**; halt the COF / movement around the gun; check whether the **muzzle** is pointed in a safe direction.
2. **Isolate:** instruct the shooter — hands to the side, step back; do not touch the gun; clear spectators from the danger sector.
3. **Safe pickup:** the **RO** / authorised person lifts the pistol by the frame or slide (no pressure on the trigger), **muzzle** kept toward the berm at all times; then unload and chamber check in the **RM**'s view if possible.
4. **Document:** issue the **DQ** under **10.5.3** or **10.5.14** depending on context; return the gun to the case / holster only after a confirmed safe state and **RM** instruction.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* People first — if the muzzle points at the crowd, evacuate the line of sight first.
* Ideally, a second official observes the pickup for the record.

## Common RO mistakes

* A rushed pickup risking pointing the **muzzle** at yourself or the shooter.
