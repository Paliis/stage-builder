## What this is

Data-entry errors in the **electronic scoresheet** (ESS) or software glitches that put the official result in question.

## RO algorithm

1. **Verification:** immediately after the announcement on the line, reconcile time, hits, procedurals before recording into the device.
2. **Assessment:** show the screen to the shooter to confirm the displayed values (time, points, penalties).
3. **Requirements:** obtain the shooter's signature on the tablet (or another match-defined acknowledgement) as agreement with the result per **9.11** and the match procedure.
4. **Document:** ensure the record is saved / synced; on ESS failure — paper duplicate and immediate notice to the Stats Office under **9.11**.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Finality:** changes after acknowledgement — only with the RM and an authorised stats officer, if **9.11** and the regulations allow.
* **Duplicate:** paper scoresheet as backup — per MD requirement; photo / copy — per local FPSU practice.

## Local (FPSU)

Recommended to keep a photo of the paper scoresheet with an unstable ESS.

## Common RO mistakes

* Pressing "Save" before the shooter has seen the official time and confirmed the record.
