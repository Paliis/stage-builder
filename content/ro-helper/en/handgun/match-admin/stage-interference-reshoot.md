## What this is

**Outside interference** during a COF: outsiders or animals in the danger zone, accidental contact between an **RO** and the shooter, props falling not by the shooter's fault — grounds for a **reshoot** under **8.6.4** after the **RM**'s assessment.

## RO algorithm

1. **Stop:** **STOP command** the moment the interference creates risk or distorts the fairness of the **engagement**.
2. **Safety:** unload under control; move outsiders away from the **muzzle** sector; stabilise the line.
3. **Assessment (RM):** the **RM** decides whether the interference materially affected the result and whether the shooter caused it intentionally (**8.6.4**).
4. **Decision (Reshoot/DQ):** if **outside interference** is confirmed — mandatory **reshoot**; do not apply a **DQ** instead of a **reshoot** unless there are separate grounds via the **RM**.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Contact with the RO:** an accidental nudge by the official during shooter movement is the textbook **8.6.4** scenario.
* **Casing:** another shooter's casing landing on the competitor may not be **outside interference** — the **RM** decides.

## Common RO mistakes

* Refusing a **reshoot** when the shooter stopped because of a real danger on the stage.
