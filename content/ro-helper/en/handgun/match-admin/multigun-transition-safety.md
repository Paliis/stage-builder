## What this is

Unsafe handling while dropping or picking up a firearm in the **transition zone** in **multigun** under **Appendix G**.

## RO algorithm

1. **Verification:** before each transition — visually confirm a safe **muzzle** direction and WSB compliance (unloaded / safety on, etc.).
2. **Assessment:** ensure the shooter is not holding two firearms in their hands at once if WSB forbids it.
3. **Requirements:** if the **muzzle** breaks the safe angles — **"STOP!"**; resolve the violation before continuing.
4. **Document:** gross safety violations are processed by the RM under Section 10 and **Appendix G**.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Empty gun:** if WSB requires the firearm to be empty in the transition — a chambered round when set down is a critical violation; the RM qualifies it.
* **Baskets / racks:** the structure must hold the **muzzle** in a safe direction; if not — escalate to the MD before shooting.

## Local (FPSU)

Chapter XIII. Control of "dry" manipulations during platform changes — safety first.

## Common RO mistakes

* Not checking the state of the "abandoned" firearm before the shooter has finished the stage.
