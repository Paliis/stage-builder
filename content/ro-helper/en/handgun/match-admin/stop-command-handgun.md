## What this is

Applying **8.3.2** during a handgun COF: **STOP command**, safe unloading, **RM** report, decision on **reshoot** (**8.6.4**) or sanctions (**10.6.1** for unsporting acts, in particular after **STOP**).

## RO algorithm

1. **Stop:** **STOP command**; if possible, a brief touch on the shooter's shoulder without disturbing safe gun control.
2. **Safety:** **muzzle** at the targets / berm; finger off the trigger guard; remove the magazine, chamber check, controlled hammer-down into the berm only per match procedure; no holstering until empty is confirmed.
3. **Assessment (RM):** report the cause of the stop (danger, suspected squib, **RO** error, **outside interference**).
4. **Decision (Reshoot/DQ):** **reshoot** for an **8.6.4** event or an erroneous stop; a **DQ** / other sanctions for confirmed **unsporting conduct** or **10.6.1** — **RM** only.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Squib:** any suspicion of a stuck bullet — **STOP** is mandatory; further actions only with the **RM**.
* **Engagement:** do not allow movement back along the stage with a pistol in hand after **STOP**.

## Common RO mistakes

* Letting the shooter holster the pistol before a chamber check.
