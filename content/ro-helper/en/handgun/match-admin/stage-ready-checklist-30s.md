## What this is

A short **checklist** before the squad's first shooter: the stage matches the WSB and **10.5**; the **8.3.1** commands can be issued only when ready.

## RO algorithm

1. **Targets / steel:** paper pasted; steel in WSB state; penalty (No-Shoot) targets are not "bare".
2. **Activators:** moving targets and poppers in start position; the **engagement** path is clear of stray objects.
3. **Safety:** no one in the danger sectors; the participant's **muzzle** will be controlled per the COF after the start.
4. **Timer / tablet:** the **timer** is on, battery OK; ready for crisp **8.3.1** commands.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* Even one item not OK — no "Load and Make Ready" (**8.3.1**).
* **Recoil reaction** from neighbouring stages does not waive the requirement of a clean bay and people in safety (**10.5**).

## Common RO mistakes

* A start with an un-pasted penalty target or an unset activator.
