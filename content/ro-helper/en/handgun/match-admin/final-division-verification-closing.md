## What this is

Final verification of classes / divisions and statuses in the system before signing the final results — **9.10** only; data safety is coordinated with the **MD** (**10.5** for incidents that left a DQ).

## RO algorithm

1. **Report:** list of open Stats issues (timing, gear, No Score).
2. **Reconciliation:** declared division vs. actual gear — per **MD** procedure, recorded under **9.10**.
3. **Statuses:** DQ / DNF / No Score must have confirmation in the match log for **9.10**.
4. **Sign-off:** the **RM** locks the **9.10** final; after the verification deadline, changes only by **MD** rules.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* Minimum division size — per **MD** primary rules, not in this card.
* After **9.10** finalisation, class appeals only as the **MD** allows.

Chapter VI. Export to the national rating after **9.10** close.
