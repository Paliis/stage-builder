## What this is

Organising the staff who do **stage restoration** (pasting, painting steel, resetting targets) under **7.1.9**. 2026 logic: **helpers may not advise the shooter** — no hints about targets or tactics.

## RO algorithm

1. **Assessment:** after scoring and recording, check that targets and steel are ready for the next shooter; that **helpers** are in safe zones during the COF.
2. **Action:** clear authorisation for **stage restoration** only after the official scoring of the string is complete; during the COF — only movement outside the firing lines.
3. **Communicate:** brief: no talking with the shooter during their stage; no gestures or comments that could affect the COF.
4. **Document:** if the shooter contests a target's state — do not let **helpers** touch the disputed target until the **RO** / **RM** decides; record the state for the report.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* Phrases like "one target left" are outside assistance — stop the line and pass it to the **RM**.
* **Helpers** wear **eye protection** and **active ear protection** / plugs on the line as required by the **MD**.

## Local (FPSU)

Chapter VI. The stage **RO** is responsible for the quality of **stage restoration**; helper errors are escalated to the **RM**.
