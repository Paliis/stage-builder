## What this is

Collecting and preserving physical evidence for the **arbitration committee** during an **appeal** under **11.1** and per the evidence requirements of **11.6**.

## RO algorithm (legal protocol)

1. **Identify:** once an **appeal** is announced under **11.1**, freeze the disputed targets, casings, and video positions — no changes without an **RM** order.
2. **Document:** mark the target (shooter ID, stage, time), remove it from its mount carefully; draft a brief incident report for the committee; official match recordings are the priority evidence under **11.6**.
3. **Escalate:** hand the evidence package to the **RM** / arbitration committee chair with a transfer log; private shooter video is admissible only if the primary rules and **11.6** allow.
4. **Record decision:** archive evidence per **MD** policy after the committee rules; update the **official scoreboard** and inform the shooter in writing.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* A photo on the stand is supporting material; the physical target is normally the key piece of evidence for **engagement** near zone borders.
* Helpers do not paste the target before the **RM** inspection while an **appeal** is pending.

## Common RO mistakes

* Letting the target be pasted before the official inspection while the **appeal** is open.
