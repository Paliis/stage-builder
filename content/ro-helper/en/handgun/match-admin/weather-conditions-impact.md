## What this is

Extreme weather (heavy rain, strong wind) that disrupts **stage restoration**, props, or makes the **engagement** unsafe — assessed under **2.1.1**.

## RO algorithm

1. **Assessment:** target / popper damage, uncontrolled prop motion, slippery surface after rain; whether the COF can safely continue.
2. **Action:** on critical risk — **STOP** on the line, halt firing, ensure **muzzle control**; do not force the shooter to finish on unstable targets.
3. **Communicate:** notify the **RM** / **MD**; announce a pause for **stage restoration** or condition change (sand, mats) by their decision.
4. **Document:** record the **RM**'s decision on a **reshoot** if the conditions during a string were unfair or unsafe; time and brief description of the event.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* Target falls / equipment glitches due to wind — escalate to the **RM** for qualification and possible **reshoot**, no other-chapter references in this card.
* A slippery **stage surface** and its restoration are coordinated with the **RM** per the match's primary rules.

## Common RO mistakes

* Allowing **engagement** of targets falling or swinging outside the stage's intended scenario.
