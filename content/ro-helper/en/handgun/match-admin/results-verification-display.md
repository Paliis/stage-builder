## What this is

A discrepancy between published results and what was recorded on the stage, or a breach of the verification procedure under **9.11** and **11.1**.

## RO algorithm

1. **Verification:** give the shooter access to their result on the display (tablet, scoreboard, official online service) per **9.11**.
2. **Assessment:** if an error is reported, compare the ESS record with the paper duplicate or the stage log if maintained.
3. **Requirements:** technical fixes (time, line transposition) are made by an authorised person on RM decision under **9.11**; a rule-interpretation dispute goes through **11.1**.
4. **Document:** after the verification window defined by **11.1** and the match regulations, the results are considered final for the corresponding stage.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Publication:** the moment of official publication is set by the regulations (scoreboard / portal) and **9.11**.
* **Arbitration:** issues already settled after the target close / signature and the protest procedure are not re-opened "from scratch" without grounds in **11.1**.

## Local (FPSU)

Protest deadlines and the Stats role — per the MD briefing and national competition rules.

## Common RO mistakes

* Refusing to show the ESS record or the stage log after the stage ends without an RM instruction.
