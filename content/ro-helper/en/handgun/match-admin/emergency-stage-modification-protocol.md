## What this is

A critical **10.5** threat (ricochet, unsafe **engagement** geometry) that requires a stage stop and modification under **RM** control.

## RO algorithm

1. **Stop:** "Stop" (**8.3.1**); firing line is safe; **muzzle** under control.
2. **RM:** report to **RM** / **MD**; **10.5** risk assessment; no firing until decision.
3. **Modification:** target / prop adjustment on the **RM**'s order; brief every squad.
4. **9.10:** void prior stage results and mandatory reshoot for shooters who already shot — by **RM** decision, recorded under **9.10**.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* New shooters do not start until the **RM** announces the updated WSB and **10.5** safety.
* Stage cancellation is the last resort — only if a safe configuration is impossible.

## Common RO mistakes

* Continuing to send shooters after a geometry change without re-shooting the previous ones.
