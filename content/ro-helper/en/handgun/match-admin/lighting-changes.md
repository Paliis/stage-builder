## What this is

A material change in target / stage visibility (dusk, fog, artificial light failure) within the scope of **2.1.1**. 2026 logic: stopping the stage due to a **lighting change** is decided by the **RM only**, not by the **RO** alone.

## RO algorithm

1. **Assessment:** is safe and fair target / reference visibility preserved for the current shooter compared with others in the squad.
2. **Action:** if visibility has critically degraded — **temporarily halt** sending shooters to the firing line until the **RM**'s instruction (do not declare a stage "closure" on your own).
3. **Communicate:** call the **RM** at once / hand the situation to the **MD** per the match procedure; brief the shooters on the pause.
4. **Document:** carry out the **RM**'s decision (continue, **stage restoration**, **reshoot** for affected strings, etc.); record the time and conditions.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Equality:** identical visibility within a squad / division is the priority for the **RM**'s evaluation.
* **Shadows:** a natural **lighting change** without loss of safe target visibility normally does not require a stop in this scenario.

## Common RO mistakes

* Voiding results or assigning a **reshoot** because of light without an **RM** decision.
