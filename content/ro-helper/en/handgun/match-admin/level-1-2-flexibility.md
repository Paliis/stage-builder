## What this is

Applying IPSC rules at a **club match** and **Level I–II** competitions with operational and equipment flexibility under **1.1.5** and the competition structure of **6.1.1**, without compromising safety.

## RO algorithm

1. **Identify:** confirm the match level (**Level I–II**) and the shooter's experience; adapt the briefing tempo at a **club match** without lowering the requirements for **engagement** in safe sectors only.
2. **Lead:** explain the WSB in plain language: commands, firing lines, **muzzle control**; avoid excess "legalese" if it does not interfere with applying the rules equally to everyone.
3. **Flexibility:** allow minor equipment deviations if the **MD** / **Level I** regulations permit and there is no advantage or risk; division/technical questions are settled with the **RM** before the start.
4. **Safety:** respond to safety violations in full per the primary rules (**DQ**, **STOP**) — no "match-level leniency"; **recoil reaction** and **muzzle** direction are always under full **RO** control.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* At **Level I**, stages may not pass full certification as at a championship — the **RO** personally checks safety sectors and **stage restoration**.
* The point of **Level I** is onboarding — flexibility applies to service and education, not to safety rules.

## Common RO mistakes

* A harsh "disciplinary" reading of a beginner's minor technical deviations at a first **club match**, when the issue can be solved before the start without risk.
