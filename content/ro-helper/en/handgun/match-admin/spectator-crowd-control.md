## What this is

Spectators or shooters in queue too close to the **engagement** sector, breach of silence and safety boundaries — venue layout under **2.1.1** (competition conditions / safe environment).

## RO algorithm

1. **Assessment:** confirm all outsiders are behind the spectator line / outside the danger sector; the queue does not stall in the **RO**'s working area.
2. **Action:** politely require people to step back to a safe distance; for filming — only from positions that do not interfere with judging or create risk.
3. **Communicate:** remind spectators not to coach the shooter; children and pets only under adult supervision and with hearing protection if the **MD** requires it.
4. **Document:** if instructions are refused — call the **RM** / **MD** to remove the person from the bay; record the incident.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* A **reshoot** is normally not required if the breach did not affect the string — the priority is to stop unsafe behaviour before the **engagement**.
* Photo/video must not force the **RO** to leave a safe escort position.

## Local (FPSU)

Chapter II. Follow national restrictions on spectator and queue conduct as approved by the **MD**.
