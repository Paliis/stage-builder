## What this is

Closing out the stats office with full **9.10** data and a **Zero Error** procedure when finalising stages: not a single "loose-end" squad in the system without an **RM** / Stats **7.1** signature.

## RO algorithm

1. **Standard: preparation:** collect tablets / media; check sync; reconcile electronic records with paper duplicates if the primary rules require it under **9.10**.
2. **Standard: execution (Zero Error):** for each stage: all squads closed, no "pending" shooters, DQ/DNF flagged; double-check totals on a random sample or full list per **MD** policy under **9.10**.
3. **Standard: coordination:** any electronic vs. paper mismatch — pause the stage close until the **RM** and Stats **7.1** decide; **RO** provides the scoresheet and explanation.
4. **Standard: documentation:** archive scoresheets, back up the database, report to **MD** / region; destroy temporary copies only after a verified **9.10** backup.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* Paper retention period — per **MD** primary rules and **9.10** (often up to a year for disputes).
* Every **DQ** must include the rule reference and the time of issue for the **9.10** audit.

Chapter IX. Upload to the national rating system within the deadline set by the federation **MD**.
