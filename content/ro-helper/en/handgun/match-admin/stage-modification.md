## What this is

Technical impossibility of running the stage in its initial form due to equipment damage or changed conditions.

## RO algorithm

1. **Detection:** record the defect that makes the stage non-equal for all shooters (e.g., a moved barricade).
2. **Stop:** temporarily halt firing on the stage and notify the RM.
3. **Fix:** the RM decides on stage restoration or modification under 2.3.
4. **Restore:** after the fix — Reshoot or another consequence per the RM decision for the shooters whose result was affected.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Immutability:** once the first shooter has officially started the stage, design changes are allowed only on critical safety grounds or via the 2.3 procedure with the RM.
* **Cancellation:** if the RM cannot restore the stage to a fair state, the stage may be voided or amended per the match regulations.

## Local (FPSU)

Operational consequences (reshoot, run cancellation) — per MD regulations and RM decision.

## Common RO mistakes

* Trying to "tweak" props yourself during the squad's run without the RM.
