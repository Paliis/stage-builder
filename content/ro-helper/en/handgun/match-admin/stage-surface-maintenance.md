## What this is

Degradation of the stage surface and ground (slipping, holes, casing piles) raises the risk of falls and affects **engagement** safety — controlled under **2.1.5** (stage surface / safe practicability).

## RO algorithm

1. **Assessment:** before each string and between shooters, inspect movement areas: casings, mud, water, ice; whether conditions are equal for the squad.
2. **Action:** organise casing pickup, level the ground; if needed, request sand, mats, gravel from **MD** / range staff before the match continues.
3. **Communicate:** warn shooters about a temporary pause for **stage restoration**; coordinate with the **RM** if the surface is systemically unsafe (mass slipping).
4. **Document:** record time and measures; on **RM** decision — **reshoot** if unfair conditions already affected a string.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Recoil reaction** and abrupt direction changes on a slippery surface raise the risk of losing **muzzle control** — safety beats restoration speed.
* The first and the last shooter in a division should have comparable surface quality within reason.

## Common RO mistakes

* Ignoring casing piles on the shooter's movement line until an incident.
