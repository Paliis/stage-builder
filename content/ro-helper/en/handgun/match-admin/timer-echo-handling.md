## What this is

2026 logic: spurious **timer** triggers from **echo** or neighbouring shots are corrected to a fair time for **9.10**.

## RO algorithm

1. **Data:** review the split times after the COF; count the shots and compare with the actual string.
2. **Verification:** if the last impulse comes "after silence" or is anomalously long — likely an **echo**, not the end of the stage.
3. **Correction:** discard the spurious impulse with the shooter's agreement; on disagreement — **RM** and a reshoot decision (**9.10** records the final).
4. **Prevention:** mic sensitivity, angle to the **muzzle**, **timer** screen visible during movement.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* Do not record a "phantom" time — it distorts Hit Factor in **9.10**.
* Loud ranges: a shorter pause between the readiness check (**8.3.1**) and the start cuts the chance of an outside sound.

## Common RO mistakes

* Treating an **echo** as the legitimate last shot.
