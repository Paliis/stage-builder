## What this is

Distinguishing the scoring diameter of a hit from a paper **radial tear** under **9.5.4** and the **Appendix B** templates.

## RO algorithm

1. **Inspect:** assess the hole's contact with the zone line; apply an overlay calibre if the **MD** allows.
2. **Assess:** 2026 logic: a **radial tear** that only touches the line without the **grease ring** / main bullet channel on the line does **not** upgrade the zone under **9.5.4**. The score is decided by the **grease ring** or a continuous bullet-pass diameter.
3. **Announce:** call the lower zone if only a "crack" crosses the line; the higher zone only when the **grease ring** touches the line.
4. **Document:** record on the scoresheet; for a protest — preserve the target.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* Vibration cracks from neighbouring shots are not hits.
* The **muzzle** angle and the **engagement** angle explain elongated tears — rely on the **grease ring**.

## Common RO mistakes

* Crediting an "Alpha" when only a long crack crossed the line without the **grease ring** on the boundary.
