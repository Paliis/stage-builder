## What this is

A short scaffold of **RO** work: target types under **4.1** / **4.2**, the Universal Target **scoring zone**, **pasting** only after the result is recorded (**9.1.3**), and **restoration** after the previous shooter (**9.1.4**).

## RO algorithm

1. **Inspect:** walk the target sequence with the shooter; point out the holes; verify there are no extraneous holes (**9.1.4**) and that **pasting** is forbidden until the inspection is complete (**9.1.3**).
2. **Assess:** decide zones based on the clear borders of **4.1** / **4.2**; for paper, use the **grease ring** as the reference for a full-calibre hole within the **scoring zone**.
3. **Announce:** clearly call out the zones and misses; give the shooter a moment to appeal to the **RM** before **pasting**.
4. **Document:** enter into the scoresheet / ESS exactly what was called; on a **9.1.3** breach or insurmountable uncertainty under **9.1.4** — the **RM** decides on a **reshoot**.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Doubles:** two shots "in the same hole" — look for two edges of the hole or separate **grease rings**; otherwise **RM**.
* **Recoil reaction:** during the inspection, do not let the shooter touch the target — contact can alter the evidence.

## Local (FPSU)

Chapter IX. The shooter must not point at holes with a finger or any object near a **DQ** boundary — use the **RO**'s voice cues.
