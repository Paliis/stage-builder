## What this is

A steel target stayed standing after the shooter engaged it. The outcome is decided per **9.5.7** and, if needed, the match's calibration / REF procedure.

## RO algorithm

1. **Inspect (do not touch!):** visually record the target's state and the absence of any contact / interference before deciding. If anyone touches the target before the inspection — a reshoot is processed per match procedure.
2. **Verify:** establish whether there are signs of an equipment failure (REF) or grounds for calibration per match procedure; call the RM if needed.
3. **Announce:** if the target is in working order — score a **Miss** under **9.5.7**. If REF is confirmed or the match procedure requires a check — declare a reshoot.
4. **Document:** record the decision (Miss / reshoot) on the scoresheet and a brief note of the basis (REF / calibration / no fall).

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Fact of fall:** for steel, the decisive factor is the actual fall / activation, not "the ring" or assumption.
* **No interference:** do not let anyone lift / move the steel until the decision is closed.

## Common RO mistakes

* Crediting a hit "by the ring" without an actual fall or without the verification procedure.
