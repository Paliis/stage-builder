## What this is

Quick and transparent results recording at a **club match** while honouring the scoresheet and protest rules under **9.7** and **9.11** (Jan 2026).

## RO algorithm

1. **Entry:** record the time and hits in ESS / scoring app or on the paper scoresheet immediately after the string; verify the binding to the correct shooter and stage.
2. **Verification:** show the shooter the totals (zones, procedurals, time) before pasting the target; let them flag an obvious entry error on the spot.
3. **Backup:** maintain a paper backup row or a squad-notebook duplicate when ESS connectivity is at risk so the data can be restored under **9.7**.
4. **Document:** signature / acknowledgement in the app; publish interim standings per **MD** policy; pass disputes to the **RM** under the **9.11** procedure.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* Transparency: the shooter should understand misses and penalties before **stage restoration**, when this is possible without delaying safety.
* At a **club match** the **RM**'s scoring decision is often final within **9.11**, with no formal fee if the regulations say so.
