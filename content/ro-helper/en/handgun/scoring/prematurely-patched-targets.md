## What this is

A **scoring target** or **No-Shoot** is **pasted** / painted before the **RO** has recorded and announced the shooter's result — a breach of **9.1.3** (2026 logic: **reshoot**).

## RO algorithm

1. **Inspect:** detect **pasting** by a helper or another person before the inspection / "Range is Clear" command per match procedure.
2. **Assess:** decide whether the holes can be reliably reconstructed; if not — qualify as a **9.1.3** violation.
3. **Announce:** immediately tell the shooter that the score is invalid; call the **RM**.
4. **Document:** the **RM** orders a full-stage **reshoot**; time and points of the **9.1.3**-affected attempt are voided; no penalties are added to the shooter for this.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Partial pasting:** even a single prematurely pasted target can make a fair score impossible — decision by the **RM** under **9.1.3**.
* **Engagement:** do not "guess" the score from a mark under the patch instead of a **reshoot**.

## Common RO mistakes

* Letting helpers paste targets while the shooter is still inspecting the result.
