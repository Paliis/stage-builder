## What this is

A **scoring target** or **No-Shoot** does not match the types and dimensions in **4.1** / **4.2** (in particular Universal Target): unclear **scoring zone** borders, smudged A/C/D lines.

## RO algorithm

1. **Inspect:** before the start, check perforation, print, and visibility of **scoring zone** borders; No-Shoot — contrasting background / "X" marking per **4.1** / **4.2**.
2. **Assess:** if a hit cannot be objectively scored due to print defect or worn lines — require target replacement before any **engagement**.
3. **Announce:** notify MD / helpers about the swap; tell the shooters the stage will not start until targets are compliant.
4. **Document:** log stage readiness in the bay journal / briefing; on doubt about the cardboard back, do not credit hits from a side not foreseen by match rules (**4.1** / **4.2**).

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Universal Target:** the outer silhouette geometry may vary, but the A, C, D **scoring zones** must remain unambiguous for the RO.
* **Recoil reaction:** do not mix target inspection with **muzzle** direction control on the line.

## Common RO mistakes

* Crediting hits when zone lines are covered with patches from previous runs and the **scoring zone** boundary is unreadable.
