## What this is

Steel **scoring targets** that do not match the types and safety requirements of **4.1** / **4.2**: dimensions, surface state (cratering, sharp burrs), faulty popper drop.

## RO algorithm

1. **Inspect:** check the face for "cup" pitting, cracks, loose mounts; verify the type (classic / mini, etc.) against **4.1** / **4.2** and the WSB.
2. **Assess:** if the **recoil reaction** of fragments or ricochet becomes unpredictable — pull the target off the line for repair.
3. **Announce:** assign helpers to paint after each run for hit visibility if the MD provides for it; forbid the start with unsafe steel.
4. **Document:** log the equipment state at the start of the shift; after replacement — confirm readiness for **engagement**.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Popper:** the mechanism must reliably fall when hit in the steel **scoring zone** defined by the target type under **4.1** / **4.2**.
* **Muzzle:** the RO does not fix steel during a COF — only **STOP** and the MD.

## Local (FPSU)

Chapter VI §4. Steel without confirmed material specs is not allowed at competitions — coordinate with the MD.
