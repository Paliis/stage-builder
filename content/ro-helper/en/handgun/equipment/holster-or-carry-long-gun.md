## What this is

Placement of the firearm or magazines outside the zones permitted by the division rules.

## RO algorithm

1. **Inspect:** measure the distance from the body to the firearm grip and verify the position relative to the hip bones (if needed).
2. **Assess:** detect a mismatch with the division's requirements (e.g. Standard/Production).
3. **Action:** order the shooter to bring the equipment into spec by "Load and Make Ready".
4. **Document:** if the mismatch is found during the stage — call the RM for a move to Open.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Distance:** the maximum distance from the inner side of the belt to the firearm grip — 50 mm (5.2.5).
* **Exceptions:** in the Open division the holster and belt-pouch position limits do not apply.
