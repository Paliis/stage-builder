## What this is

The configuration does not match **Appendix D4a**: **optic** mounting, pistol model, or **trigger** pull weight.

## RO algorithm

1. **Inspect:** confirm that the **optical sight** is mounted on the moving part (the **slide** moves together with the sight under **recoil**) per **Appendix D4a**.
2. **Assess:** absence of a forbidden frame mount on the fixed frame, if **Appendix D4a** rules it out.
3. **Action:** **trigger** pull weight — not below the **Appendix D4a** / Production requirements (target **2.27 kg** — per the appendix).
4. **Document:** on a mismatched mount or model — RM for a move to **Open** or to fix the breach.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Mounting:** the sight must move synchronously with the **slide**; check play and mount safety after chrono / firing tests.
* **Approved list:** the model must be permitted for **Production** / **Production Optics** per **Appendix D4a** and the IPSC list.

## Local (FPSU)

Appendix 4. **Optic** mount reliability is a safety priority; cracked mounts — stop until the RM inspects.

## Common RO mistakes

* Allowing a sight mounted on the fixed frame in a class where **Appendix D4a** requires mounting on the **slide**.
