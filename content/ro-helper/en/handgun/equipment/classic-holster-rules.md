## What this is

Equipment placement of a **Classic** shooter that breaches the anatomical limits and the **Appendix D** requirements alongside **5.2.7**.

## RO algorithm

1. **Inspect:** the shooter is in a relaxed stance; the RO visually checks the hip-bone line and the position of the holster and pouches on the **gun belt**.
2. **Assess:** in **Classic** the holster and pouches must be **behind** the hip-bone crest; the unified **50 mm** check (from the body to the grip / equipment) applies to divisions per **5.2.7** and **Appendix D** — **Open** is excluded from this limit.
3. **Action:** before "Load and Make Ready" order the equipment to be moved back or the distance brought into spec.
4. **Document:** if the breach is after the start or correction is refused — RM for the decision (most often a move to a division with the matching **Appendix D**, e.g. **Open**).

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Muzzle angle:** the holster must hold the pistol so that the muzzle stays in a safe sector by the shooter's feet during movement.
* **Front pouch:** during **engagement** and rapid position changes, verify nothing "rides" forward of the hip line.

## Common RO mistakes

* Ignoring a front pouch that, dynamically, ends up forward of the permitted line.
