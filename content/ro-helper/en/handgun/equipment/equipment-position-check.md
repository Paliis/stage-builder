## What this is

Holster or pouches placed forward of the hip-bone line where **5.2.7** forbids it for the division.

## RO algorithm

1. **Inspect:** the shooter stands upright, arms down; the RO visually marks the line of the most prominent point of the hip bones.
2. **Assess:** the entire pistol in the holster and the pouches must be **behind** that line for the divisions covered by **5.2.7**.
3. **Action:** order the equipment moved back before "Load and Make Ready".
4. **Document:** if the breach is during the stage — RM for a move to **Open** or sanction per regulations.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Divisions:** **Standard**, **Production**, **Production Optics**, **Classic** — check **5.2.7** alongside the corresponding class **Appendix D**.
* **Exception:** in **Open** and **Revolver** the belt position is normally not restricted by **5.2.7** the same way as in the narrow divisions — verify against the rules text.

## Local (FPSU)

Chapter VII §2. For a uniform check, the shooter may be asked to press the palms to the thighs to define the hip-bone crest.

## Common RO mistakes

* Eyeballing without an RM-agreed hip line on the venue.
