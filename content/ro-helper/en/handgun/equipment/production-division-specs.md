## What this is

The pistol does not meet **Appendix D4**: model outside the **IPSC Approved** list, external changes, **barrel**, **trigger** pull weight, or starting magazine load.

## RO algorithm

1. **Inspect:** cross-check the model against the official list and **Appendix D4** requirements.
2. **Assess:** by regulation — check the **trigger** pull weight (minimum **2.27 kg** for the first shot — per **Appendix D4**).
3. **Action:** verify the **barrel** length and absence of disallowed external modifications; the starting magazine load — per **Appendix D4**.
4. **Document:** on a mismatch — RM for a move to **Open** or to fix the breach.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Magazine:** the cartridge limit at the start of a stage — per **Appendix D4** (target **15** — confirm in the current PDF).
* **Tape patches:** permitted zones for anti-slip materials — only as in **Appendix D4**.

## Local (FPSU)

Appendix 4. Replacement of mechanical sights with equivalent factory types — provided they meet **Appendix D4**.

## Common RO mistakes

* Banning the swap of **rear sight / front sight** to another mechanical pair of the same classification without grounds in **Appendix D4**.
