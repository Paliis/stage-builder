## What this is

Firearm does not match the declared **Open** division per **Appendix D1**. An unsafe configuration is processed per match procedure (up to a start halt); other mismatches — move to a permitted division or "outside scoring" by the RM's decision.

## RO algorithm

1. **Inspect:** review the **optic**, compensator, magazine length, belt position per **Appendix D1**.
2. **Assess:** if needed — measure the magazine with the official template (target **170 mm** — verify the methodology in **Appendix D1**).
3. **Action:** confirm **Major/Minor** compliance from chronograph results and match regulations.
4. **Document:** record the **Open** division in the protocol; on a dispute — RM.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Modifications:** the permitted set of changes is defined by **Appendix D1** and general safety rules; the **barrel**, **slide**, and **trigger** must remain safe through manipulation.
* **Magazine:** length is measured per the **Appendix D1** rules (with the basepad, if the appendix so provides).

## Local (FPSU)

Appendix 4. The holster position in **Open** is not restricted by the Standard/Production position rules — do not "invent" limits beyond **Appendix D1**.

## Common RO mistakes

* Trying to regulate the **Open** holster position by the criteria of the narrow divisions.
