## What this is

Breach of the holster, speed-loader, and accessory placement rules for **Revolver** under **Appendix D** and **5.2.7** (where applicable).

## RO algorithm

1. **Inspect:** review the **gun belt**, holster, and loading devices; cross-check **Appendix D** for **Revolver**.
2. **Assess:** the "behind hip bones" limit for narrow divisions does not apply to **Revolver** the same way as to **Classic**; focus on the **unified 50 mm check** from the inner side of the belt to the firearm and ammo (except where **Open** / **Appendix D** text changes it).
3. **Action:** require secure ammo retention during movement; correct the position or mounts before the stage start.
4. **Document:** confirm division compliance or call the RM on doubts about **Appendix D** interpretation.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Placement freedom:** speed loaders / moon clips may sit anywhere on the belt per **Appendix D**, provided the safety and distance requirements are met.
* **Recoil reaction:** an unreliable mount raises the risk of losing control during a string.

## Local (FPSU)

Appendix 4. The **magnetic holder** for moon clips must be rigidly secured to the belt; a weak mount is grounds to require replacement before the start.
