## What this is

**Sections 5.1–5.8** of **IPSC Handgun** describe requirements for the **pistol** (configuration, permitted modifications, trigger, magazine well etc.) and the **ammunition** (bullet type, minimum energy / PF after the chrono, forbidden constructions). The RO on the line spots **obvious** breaches; precise thresholds and measurements are at the **chronograph** and with **arbiters** under the **MD**.

## What the RO does

1. **Visually verify** division compliance (compensator, optic, magazine length — per **Chapter 5** and the Appendix).
2. **Refuse the start** with suspicious ammunition (e.g. clearly the wrong calibre) — **RM**.
3. **Direct to the chronograph** before a powder / bullet change if regulations require a re-check of PF.
4. **Remember** safety: a damaged chamber, frame cracks — **RM**, possible halt of fire.
5. **Do not give technical findings** instead of the division arbiter.

## IPSC (Jan 2026)

- Read **5.1–5.8** in full; cross-reference **10.5** / **10.4** if the breach becomes unsafe.

## Common mistakes

- **Starting with a magazine** forbidden in the division.
- **Ignoring** an obviously damaged firearm.
- **The RO measures PF** without qualification or a calibrated chronograph.
