## What this is

The revolver or accessories do not meet **Appendix D5** (sight, compensator, cylinder, speed-loading devices, etc.).

## RO algorithm

1. **Inspect:** review the **barrel**, cylinder, presence of ports and compensators per **Appendix D5**.
2. **Assess:** permissibility of **moon clips** / speed loaders — per **Appendix D5** and the WSB.
3. **Action:** make sure the no-reload string does not breach the stage requirements and **Appendix D5** (rounds-before-reload — per the WSB).
4. **Document:** record the **Revolver** division; modification dispute — RM.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Optics:** forbidden in **Revolver** per **Appendix D5** — otherwise division change by RM decision.
* **Ports / compensator:** permissibility is set by **Appendix D5**; do not transfer **Open** rules without checking the appendix.

## Local (FPSU)

Cylinder and trigger mechanism safety check — per MD procedure.

## Common RO mistakes

* Hard-capping the cylinder round count without basis in the WSB and **Appendix D5**.
