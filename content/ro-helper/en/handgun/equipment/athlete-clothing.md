## What this is

**5.3** of **IPSC Handgun** sets requirements for the shooter's **clothing** at a match: footwear, gloves, advertising / logos, number visibility, etc. The aim — **safety** (stability on uneven ground), **identification**, and **neutrality** about sponsors. Details — **only from the Jan 2026 PDF** and the **MD** (extra venue prohibitions).

## What the RO does

1. **Check** at registration / before the start what is visible on the line (number, obviously unsafe footwear, forbidden text — per the rules).
2. **Do not DQ** for "style" beyond the rules text — disputed cases to the **RM**.
3. **Remind** about **MD** requirements (e.g. covered shoulders in certain zones) if announced in the briefing.
4. **During the COF** clothing is rarely a stop ground — except when it creates a **hazard** (laces in the trigger etc.) — then **STOP** and an **RM** assessment.
5. **Respect** equality: the same standards for every competitor.

- **Comments on appearance** instead of citing a rule.
- **Ignoring** unsafe footwear on wet ground.
