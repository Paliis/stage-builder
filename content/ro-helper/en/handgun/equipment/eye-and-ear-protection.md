## What this is

**5.4** requires shooters (and often staff in the firing zone) to wear **eye protection** and **hearing protection** per the PDF text: glasses type (coverage, side shield), muffs vs plugs, etc. This is **safety**; breaches may halt fire or trigger a **DQ** — depending on the rule wording and the **MD**.

## What the RO does

1. **Verify** before the start that the shooter is wearing **glasses** and **muffs/plugs** (as the match requires).
2. **Stop** the prep if protection is removed on the line during nearby active fire.
3. **The RO themselves** must always be in protection in a "hot" zone — set the example.
4. **Remind** spectators / coaches outside the permitted area if they enter a risk zone.
5. **Escalate** to the **RM** if the shooter refuses to put protection on after a warning.

## Common mistakes

- **Starting without glasses** "for a second" during set-up.
- **Sunglasses** without a ballistic standard, if the rules forbid that.
- **The RO without their own protection** on the line.
