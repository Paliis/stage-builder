## What this is

Magazines or loading do not meet **Appendix D3** (single stack, dimensions, round limits).

## RO algorithm

1. **Inspect:** review magazines for **single-stack** and **Appendix D3** compliance.
2. **Assess:** check the **Major/Minor** round count per **Appendix D3** (targets **8/10** — confirm in the PDF).
3. **Action:** make sure basepads and geometry do not breach the box / well per **Appendix D3**.
4. **Document:** breach of starting load or magazine type — record for a procedural consequence per Chapter 10 per match procedure; systemic breaches — move to **Open** via the RM.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Geometry:** the magazine must enter the well without "forced" widening by forbidden magwells per **Appendix D3**.
* **Foreign magazines:** **Standard** (double-stack) magazines are not allowed even "half-loaded".

## Local (FPSU)

Measurements and templates — per match regulations; the final call on dispute — RM.

## Common RO mistakes

* Allowing a double-stack magazine because it is not fully loaded.
