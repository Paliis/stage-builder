## What this is

Use of a **flashlight**, laser pointer, or other **auxiliary equipment** under **5.1.3** in a division where **Appendix D** forbids it.

## RO algorithm

1. **Inspect:** check the rail / mounts on the pistol for a **flashlight**, laser, or combined modules.
2. **Assess:** is the device functional (if the WSB requires light) or only ballast; is the laser allowed only in **Open** per **Appendix D**.
3. **Action:** laser pointers in divisions like **Production** / **Production Optics** — remove or move the shooter to a division where **Appendix D** allows such **auxiliary equipment** (typically **Open**).
4. **Document:** breach after the start — RM for division change / sanction per regulations.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Flashlight:** allowed in many divisions if **Appendix D** and **5.1.3** are not breached; do not use as a forbidden monopod-like support if that conflicts with the WSB.
* **Weight and balance:** rail / frame modules must not push the configuration outside the chosen class **Appendix D**.

## Common RO mistakes

* Allowing a laser in an optics division where the **Appendix D** for the class does not foresee a laser.
