## What this is

The firearm or magazines do not meet **Appendix D3** (1911 platform, single stack, the box, magwell limits, etc.).

## RO algorithm

1. **Inspect:** review the frame, **slide**, magazines, and well for **Appendix D3** compliance.
2. **Assess:** check the **225×150×45 mm** box and single-stack magazine criteria per **Appendix D3**.
3. **Action:** verify magwell width and other dimensions per **Appendix D3**; fix breaches before the start.
4. **Document:** RM — move to **Open** or another class on use of forbidden magazines / configuration.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Capacity:** **Major/Minor** magazine limits — per **Appendix D3** (targets **8/10** — confirm in the PDF).
* **Slide:** ports in the **slide** and other changes — only if permitted by **Appendix D3**; **recoil** does not justify forbidden modifications.

## Local (FPSU)

Appendix 4. The **1911** platform's authenticity for **Classic** is checked at the equipment check.

## Common RO mistakes

* Confusing **Standard** (double-stack) magazines with the permitted **single stack** for **Classic**.
