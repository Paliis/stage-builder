## What this is

The firearm does not meet **Appendix D2** (the box, forbidden sights/compensators/ports etc.) or the equipment position under **5.2.7**.

## RO algorithm

1. **Inspect:** the shooter places the **unloaded** firearm with the magazine inserted into the **225×150×45 mm** box (per **Appendix D2**).
2. **Assess:** the **slide** is parallel to the long side of the box; the lid closes without force; no forbidden elements per **Appendix D2**.
3. **Action:** order the breach to be fixed or the division changed before the start; holster / pouch position — per **5.2.7**.
4. **Document:** call the RM for a move to **Open** or another permitted class if the box does not close or there are forbidden modifications.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* **Internal changes:** permitted as long as they do not breach the external dimensions and **Appendix D2** requirements.
* **Holster:** position relative to the hip — per **5.2.7**; **recoil** and **slide** action do not waive the dimensional box.

## Local (FPSU)

Appendix 4. The box check is run at the equipment check or on RM/MD demand.

## Common RO mistakes

* Assessing the box with a non-standard **slide** angle or "stress" on the lid.
