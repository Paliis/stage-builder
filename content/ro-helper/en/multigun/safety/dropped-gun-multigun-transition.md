## What this is

During a transition between firearms one firearm is **dropped** and touches a surface outside a controlled basket / rack — **DQ** under **10.5.3** for the entire match at the **RM**'s decision (2026 logic).

## RO algorithm

1. **Stop:** **"STOP!"**; the shooter freezes with whichever firearm remains in their hands in a safe direction.
2. **Isolation:** forbid recovering the **dropped firearm**; keep the second firearm under **RO** control until safe unloading per procedure.
3. **Safe recovery:** the **RO** lifts the **dropped firearm**, **unloads**, **controls chamber** / magazine well as applicable by firearm type; then safely unload the firearm still in the shooter's hands on command.
4. **Document:** the **RM** records the **DQ** under **10.5.3**; brief note (outside basket / in basket with tip-over).

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* Touching the ground **outside** the designated lay-down zone = **dropped gun**.
* Before leaving, the shooter must confirm the previous firearm is stable in the basket — otherwise **10.5.3** risk.

## Local (FPSU)

Chapter XIII. A drop during transition counts as a serious preparation fault; the recovery protocol is the same.
