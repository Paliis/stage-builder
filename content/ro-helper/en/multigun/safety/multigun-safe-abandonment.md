## What this is

An unsafe firearm condition in the transition zone: the **RO** records the facts, preserves evidence, and initiates formal processing under **11.1** and the evidence rules in **11.6**; sanction (including **DQ**) is determined by the **RM** per the match's primary rules.

## RO algorithm (legal protocol)

1. **Identification:** record time, name, stage; do not touch the firearm unless needed; if threatened issue **STOP** and ensure **muzzle** control by distance and commands.
2. **Documentation:** record condition (safety, slide/bolt, magazine) with photos / witnesses; retain targets and official-camera footage when they constitute evidence under **11.6**.
3. **Escalation:** **RM** at once; if the shooter disputes sanction fairness — open the **protest** chain under **11.1** without destroying evidence.
4. **Decision logging:** enter the **RM** / arbitration-committee decision in the protocol and **official results board**; return or dispose of targets only after the case is closed.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* A shot while placing the firearm in the basket is a serious incident: safety first, then the **11.6** hearing chain.
* Open vs closed action in the basket — strictly per WSB; treat deviations as a procedural matter under **RM** decision without citing other chapters on this card.

## Local (FPSU)

Chapter XIII. Leaving a loaded firearm in an improper condition is a critical incident; documentation for the arbitration committee is mandatory.
