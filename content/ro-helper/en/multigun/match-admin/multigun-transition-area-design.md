## What this is

Setting up the weapon transition zone on a multigun stage: parameters fixed in the WSB, safety verification, and formal appeal of **MD** decisions under **11.1** (2026 logic — venue disputes run as a **protest**, not ad hoc on the line).

## RO algorithm (legal protocol)

1. **Identification:** check basket / rack placement: **muzzle** of the laid-down firearm toward the safe side, zone boundaries on the ground, structural stability; cross-check with the WSB and **MD** briefing.
2. **Documentation:** photo / sketch or stage log entry before match start; record shooter comments in writing for the **RM**.
3. **Escalation:** if a competitor challenges safety or WSB compliance — accept the **protest** under **11.1**, fee and deadlines per primary rules; do not alter the venue unilaterally after the protest is opened.
4. **Decision logging:** after the arbitration committee / **RM** decision, update the WSB or announce refusal; reflect changes on the **official results board** and in squad briefings.

## Key nuances (Jan 2026)

* Soft lining at the basket bottom reduces impact risk for a loaded firearm — part of agreed design, not a substitute for safety rules.
* The **RO** must see the firearm state in the zone without interfering with neighbouring-line **engagement**.

## Common RO mistakes

* Allowing rickety structures without written **MD** confirmation and without a path to appeal under **11.1**.
